# ScienceVision

An expert-level scientific 3D visual question-answering benchmark of 250 tasks across biology, chemistry, medical imaging, mechanical CAD and architecture; the best mean score in the main 3D setting is 0.53.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether a model can obtain evidence from native 3D scientific objects and apply domain knowledge in expert-level visual question answering, including structural reading, geometric and topological relations, occluded-target identification and measurement

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing multimodal benchmarks largely evaluate text and 2D images, while scientific 3D evaluations vary in representations and protocols across disciplines; it identifies a gap in cross-domain scientific 3D question answering with a focused comparison of evidence-access settings under deterministic answer checks

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Objects are first checked for question support and viewer access; candidate questions are prepared from templates or curated drafts, sometimes with agent assistance, then sent to domain experts who review, revise, inspect the objects and supply reference answers

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Author background**：Initiated by researchers at UniPat AI and Elorian AI; the official blog states that 66 experts across the five disciplines wrote, revised, annotated and reviewed the questions, with 482.98 recorded hours

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Review process**：Experts review targets and domain assumptions, revise questions and inspect the 3D evidence; a subsequent quality review accepts the annotations or returns them for revision

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Quality control**：Of the 250 references, 110 are recorded values, 106 are script-derived and 34 are tool-derived; the task configurations contain 54 task-specific scoring adjustments (component rubrics, aliases and numerical tolerances). A prior independent audit found underspecified targets or counting units in eight CAD questions without establishing numerical reference errors; those tasks remain in the reported benchmark and are assessed separately in a sensitivity check

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Decontamination**：The paper states that reference provenance is not a training-data exposure audit, and the available evidence does not establish that the artifacts or questions were absent from model training. The dataset on Hugging Face requires granted access

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

## Data format

**Size**：250 expert-level questions, 50 each in Biology, Chemistry, Medical Imaging, Mechanical CAD and Architecture, across 22 subdomains; 143 references are single-entry and 107 have multiple recorded entries. All 250 tasks have recorded expert inspections, 2,156 viewer operations in total excluding initial states. The code repository also states that 2,405 of 2,406 recorded expert steps carry a screenshot. There are 325 asset files in total

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Question types**：Scientific 3D visual question answering; answers may be categories, counts, coordinates, measurements, atom identities or ordered lists, with units and coordinate frames specified by the task

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Modalities**：Native 3D artifacts (for example mmCIF for proteins, CIF for crystals, NIfTI for CT, STEP for CAD assemblies and glTF for buildings), plus fixed rendered views in the 2D control setting; medical tasks pair volume renders with slices, giving twelve images in a six-view manifest

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Languages**：English (questions and response instructions)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Difficulty**：In the main comparison, the highest Harness + 3D mean task score is 0.5291 (GPT-6 Astra with Codex) and the highest Harness + 2D score is 0.2965 (Claude Fable 5.1). The paper states that the strongest observed answer score remains about one half of the available credit, and that this score allows partial answers, so it does not imply that half the questions were answered completely. No human performance baseline is provided. The highest domain mean in the main comparison is 0.6989 in Biology for Claude Fable 5.1 under Harness + 3D + MCP

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Public and hidden split**：The reported main comparison uses all 250 tasks; no separate hidden test split is described. The dataset on Hugging Face can be downloaded only after access is granted

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Task-level answer score on a 0-to-1 scale, including partial credit. Selected available repeats are averaged within each task, then tasks are weighted equally. The code repository also states that answer_score is 1.0 only when every field matches, that reports use mean@3, and that ineligible trials are reported separately and never mixed into a mean as a model failure

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Scoring**：Deterministic, task-specific matching verifies scored answer components against reference answers, including accepted equivalents and numerical tolerances, without a model judge. The same questions, response requirements and scoring rules apply to the Harness + 3D, Harness + 3D + MCP and Harness + 2D settings

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Prompt settings**：Not applicable (agent-harness evaluation). In the main comparison, GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol use Codex at maximum reasoning effort; the other ten models use Claude Code, whose effort is not recorded

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Official scripts**：https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)

**Comparability note**：Official comparison notes state that each score splices a carried-over segment with a re-run segment, and that six models take their re-run segment from a separate evaluation service; Gemini 4 Argon's carried-over segment has two runs rather than three, and three of Hunyuan-4's six-view questions have two runs. Codex and Claude Code are different harnesses, so a row is not a model-only comparison. In supplementary runs, Claude Opus 5.5 with Codex under Harness + 3D scores 0.5448 and Claude Fable 5.1 with Codex under Harness + 3D + MCP scores 0.5349, above the canonical maxima, but reasoning effort and task versions are not identical. The paper also notes that the blog rounds task means before computing domain scores, producing 0.6095 versus 0.6094 for Astra Harness + 3D in chemistry, a rounding difference rather than a new evaluation

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04087) [2](https://www.unipat.ai/blog/ScienceVision) [3](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/ScienceVision) (accessed 2026-11-11)
