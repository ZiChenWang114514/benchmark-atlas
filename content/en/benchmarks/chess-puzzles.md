# Chess Puzzles (Epoch)

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth verification snapshot 2026-11-07. Unverified is not the same as Not disclosed; the collection date is not the leaderboard update time.

Epoch Chess Puzzles tests reasoning about the best next move on 100 positions newly generated by a program. The input is FEN, not an image of the board. The important change this time is that a public task mirror and the data were found: 2.1.0 accepts SAN notation; its relation in scoring to 2.0.x must be explained from the official changelog, and the old statement that “only four characters are accepted” cannot continue to be used.

## Design goals

**测量能力**：Select the single best next move from a textual board state, focusing on spatial reasoning and planning.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**针对的不足**：New questions are generated by low-cost procedural generation, avoiding direct dependence on public chess-record question banks; this is not visual board recognition or full-game playing strength.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Construction

**材料来源**：First, 6 complete games are played at random, and then Stockfish at a random Elo simulates play; full-strength Stockfish selects positions whose unique best move was not chosen by the simulated player, excluding obvious capture blunders.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**出题背景**：Generated and evaluated by Epoch AI programs; the 5 chess players serve only as a small-sample human baseline and are not the question authors.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**审核**：After generation and filtering by Stockfish, the set received an external audit by Generality Labs; the official site calls the audit of 2026-10 Verified, and v2 changes to submission by tool and to deterministic scoring.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**质控**：Stockfish determines the unique best move; obvious mistakes such as capturing an unprotected piece, or a lower-value piece capturing a higher-value piece, are excluded.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**去污染边界**：Newly generated by an Epoch program, and not taken from an existing question bank; the specific Stockfish version used for generation cannot be verified, because the record is insufficient.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Data format

**规模与统计口径**：100 newly generated chess games.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**任务形式**：The input is an FEN position, and submit_answer submits the best move. Version 2.1.0 accepts SAN in addition to UCI origin and destination squares, and parses SAN from the position. There is also a Decision variant with the same 100 questions, in which a model that cannot write text makes one choice among the legal moves.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**模态**：文本

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**语言**：FEN is the board encoding, and the evaluation prompts are in English.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**难度**：The authors' subjective ratings run from relatively straightforward to fairly difficult; a sample test in which 5 humans each answer about 20 questions cannot be taken as a human baseline for the full question set.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**公开/保留划分**：The 100 positions and target moves are public in the official mirror puzzles.csv; the mirror is a manually refreshed snapshot of an internal repository, not an automatic live sync.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Evaluation

**指标**：Exact-match accuracy of the best move.

Sources: [1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**评分**：2.1.0 uses python-chess to parse UCI/SAN from the current position and then compare them with the best move; 2.0.x scores strings more strictly. Decision still requires an exact match to the UCI candidates.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**提示与运行设定**：The output budget of qa_agent is 1,000,000 tokens, and the threshold for compression is usually 90% of the model's context. When the public image uses up the budget, it forces an answer at the model's default effort. Internal scoring runs at the lowest effort. A run on the image cannot be treated as identical in full to the internal setting.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**脚本/运行入口**：https://github.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/blob/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/__init__.py

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**可比性**：1.x and 2.x cannot be compared directly. The authors say that 2.1.0 is comparable with 2.0.x and that old logs can be rescored, but 44 of 18,029 old samples were once misjudged because of notation. This snapshot still separates task versions and the Decision variant, so that the highest score is not chosen selectively.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epoch-research/chess-puzzles-mirror/main/bench/task/chess_puzzles/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/chess-puzzles) (accessed 2026-11-07)
