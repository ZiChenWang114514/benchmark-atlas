# MathVista

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review 2026-11-08. The access date is not the date the experiment was run; dates from historical sources keep their original record.

MathVista tests whether a model can combine reading a figure with mathematical calculation, rather than only recognizing text in the figure. It unifies 28 existing sources with three new datasets, IQTest, FunctionQA, and PaperQA, into 6,141 items, with question types spanning geometry, charts, textbooks, and figures in papers.

Sources: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Design goals

### 测量能力

Mathematical reasoning in a visual setting, combining close reading of figures with compositional mathematical calculation.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://mathvista.github.io/](https://mathvista.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 设计动机

Existing models can solve language problems, but a systematic evaluation covering visual-mathematics settings is lacking.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://mathvista.github.io/](https://mathvista.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Construction

### 材料来源

It brings together 28 existing multimodal datasets and adds three datasets, IQTest, FunctionQA, and PaperQA, covering figures, geometry, charts, illustrations from papers, and others.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://mathvista.github.io/](https://mathvista.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 命题与标注背景

Expert annotators confirm whether a question involves mathematical reasoning; the human baseline is completed separately by annotators with a high-school education or above, and those annotators must not be conflated with the people who wrote the questions.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Review process

VQA samples are first filtered automatically for question answering about numbers or quantities, then judged and voted on by three experts; labels of reasoning type are spot-checked by hand.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Quality control

VQA samples are first filtered automatically for question answering about numbers or quantities, then judged and voted on by three experts; labels of reasoning type are spot-checked by hand.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 去污染与留出

Test answers are not public; the official release restricts use of the data as a training set. Pretraining contamination from pre-existing source data cannot be ruled out on this basis.

Sources: [https://mathvista.github.io/](https://mathvista.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Data format

### 规模与统计对象

6,141 examples, from 31 source datasets.

### 难度与覆盖

Grade is recorded by grade level and analyzed across seven categories of mathematics skill: arithmetic, statistics, algebra, geometry, numerical common sense, science, and logical reasoning; the questions involve question answering on figures, geometry, word problems, textbooks, and visual question answering. A grade label is not a single scale of difficulty.

Sources: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02255) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 公开/私有与数据划分

testmini has 1,000 examples for development and validation; test has 5,141 examples for standard evaluation, and the official source explicitly will not release the test answer labels.

### 饱和判断边界

The first row of the official testmini table that is still available is 85.2%, and the first row of the test table is 65.84%; the two have different denominators and different times on the leaderboard, and saturation of the whole benchmark is not declared from this. The GPT-4V result of 49.9% on the official website is the historical baseline of the original paper.

Sources: [https://mathvista.github.io/data/results/model_scores.js](https://mathvista.github.io/data/results/model_scores.js) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://mathvista.github.io/](https://mathvista.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 污染、冲突与复现风险

The testmini labels are public, and the test answers are not public. The 31 source datasets may overlap with later model training, and a restriction to test use is not empirical proof of no contamination. The dataset card also says the test leaderboard is under construction, but the project site already has a table of test scores: current visibility of the leaderboard follows the actual table on the official site, and the note that the dataset card lags behind is kept.

Sources: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://mathvista.github.io/](https://mathvista.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 衍生实现

On 2026-07-27 the official README added a community-maintained EvalScope integration; the default is testmini and 0-shot, scored by mathematical parsing and numeric equivalence. There is still no report of agreement on the same predicted scores, and it cannot be treated as an equivalent substitute for the official scorer.

Sources: [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lupantech/MathVista/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)
