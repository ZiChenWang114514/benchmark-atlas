# AeroZoo

A benchmark and training corpus of well-posed flow problems for aerodynamic foundation models, with 3,815 time-mean solutions of the incompressible unsteady RANS equations, of which 2,816 mean-stable samples form the canonical set. The paper reports that ISLA reaches a median velocity error of 5.3% of inlet speed over four seeds on the random split.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether neural surrogates can predict CFD-quality incompressible flow in seconds for new shapes in new enclosures. Each sample poses a well-posed PDE problem in the form a solver receives it: boundary meshes grouped by condition type, prescribed values, and fluid properties. The canonical benchmark has an interior task and a boundary task, and uses geometry-disjoint frozen splits to test generalization across geometry families, enclosures, boundary-condition layouts, and Reynolds-number ranges.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing CFD datasets typically vary one design family in one flow domain, which tests interpolation within a family rather than the least an aerodynamic foundation model must do: predict solver-quality incompressible flow for unseen shapes in unseen enclosures. Existing datasets also often omit boundary conditions that they hold fixed, reducing learning to a shape-to-field map rather than the PDE solution operator.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Every sample is generated by simulation from a seed and a frozen recipe, not from wind-tunnel measurements. The solver is OpenFOAM 14 with snappyHexMesh, integrating the incompressible unsteady RANS equations with the Langtry–Menter transition SST model; the target is the time mean. Six of the seven geometry families were generated for this dataset. The Zero-to-CAD family is a farthest-point sample of 500 parts, within three strata of bluffness, from the 52,408 watertight single-body parts of the public Zero-to-CAD 100K corpus that pass simple shape gates. Generation ran from 2026-09-13 to 2026-10-02. Body sizes span 2 cm to 20 m, and the corpus includes 161 body-free ducts.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Author background**：All five authors are listed with affiliation NVIDIA, Santa Clara, California, USA; the corresponding author is Peter Sharpe. Further personal biographies are unverified.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Review process**：The paper is a preprint and does not describe independent peer review or external annotation review. Every case was posed before any mesh existed, and failed or timed-out cases were never replaced, only recorded with a status in the release manifest.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Quality control**：A sample becomes visible only after it has been written, reloaded, and compared tensor by tensor. The release manifest gives every one of the 4,360 planned cases a status: 3,815 completed, 208 failed a mesh or solver check, and 337 were still solving when generation stopped. The stability label compares the two halves of the averaging window and marks a sample mean_stable if volume-weighted velocity, pressure, and the inlet-aligned force coefficient agree within stated thresholds. Appendix C reports solver checks including mesh refinement, the first wall cell, inlet eddy viscosity, convection scheme, Courant number, and restarts. Native solver cases are retained so samples can be exported again without resolving.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Decontamination**：This is not a language-model question set, and the paper does not describe decontamination against text pretraining corpora. The 22 splits are frozen by hashing each primary body and are geometry-disjoint. The paper requires that models pretrained on AeroZoo may use only the scored split’s training part, and that reuse of settings selected on the random split full must be stated.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

## Data format

**Size**：Of 4,360 planned cases, 3,815 well-posed problems were released (23.8 billion cells in total, about 1.48 TB), plus one mascot geometry that is in no split and is not among the 3,815. Of the released samples, 2,816 (74%) are mean_stable and form the canonical benchmark. The corpus spans seven geometry families and eight enclosure configurations, with Reynolds numbers from 10^3 to 10^8. Cells per sample range from 0.6 to 24 million. On the canonical random split full, the train, validation, and test sizes are 2,011, 290, and 515.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Question types**：Well-posed three-dimensional incompressible flow boundary-value problems: given the boundary meshes, prescribed values, and fluid properties, a model predicts the time-mean solution (seven mean interior fields, plus fields at boundary-face centroids and wall shear on no-slip faces).

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Modalities**：Three-dimensional boundary meshes, face-wise prescribed values, fluid properties, interior coordinates, and volume-weighted flow fields; not natural-language dialogue or image question answering.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Languages**：Not applicable. The benchmark consists of meshes and numerical flow fields, not natural-language questions. The paper and metadata are in English.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Difficulty**：The paper concludes that even in distribution, the best model ISLA still has error about 4 times the change from a finer mesh, and remains far from solver accuracy. On test samples shared with the random split, holding out non-box enclosures, crossflow layouts, or dual-inlet layouts multiplies ISLA’s median ratio to the uniform-flow reference by 2.4, 5.5, and 9.2 respectively.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Public and hidden split**：The paper freezes 22 geometry-disjoint splits. The random split full is about 70/10/20% train/validation/test. The canonical benchmark trains, validates, and tests only on mean_stable samples. No separate hidden test set is described. The data themselves are not yet public on the stated hosting page.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Headline interior-task metrics are volume-weighted velocity error and near-field velocity error, both normalized by the reference speed, and pressure error normalized by the reference dynamic pressure. Results also report the ratio to a uniform free-stream reference and the share of samples with ratio below 1. The boundary task includes error in the force coefficient along the fastest-inlet direction, error in the full 3D load vector, and, in the appendix, surface-pressure, wall-shear, and duct total-pressure-loss errors. Five turbulence fields are optional auxiliary targets. A result must report each metric’s mean and median over the canonical test samples on which it is defined.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Scoring**：Each metric is normalized by the sample’s own scoring scales. Interior errors are weighted by cell volumes; the near field is points within one reference length of a body-face centroid. The force coefficient is integrated from surface pressure and no-slip wall shear, and the error denominator is the larger of the coefficient’s magnitude and 0.05. The free-stream reference predicts the inlet state everywhere (area-weighted prescribed inlet velocities and turbulence quantities, zero gauge pressure, and zero wall shear) and needs no training. The paper states that scoring code will be added to PhysicsNeMo and provides an excerpt of the reference implementation in Appendix E.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Prompt settings**：Not applicable as language-model prompts. For paper Table 3, Transolver and GeoTransolver were trained in PhysicsNeMo on the canonical training samples of split full (200,000 sampled interior points per step, one seed per row) and scored on all 515 canonical test samples. Models without boundary tokens receive only query coordinates and signed distance to no-slip walls; GeoTransolver with boundary tokens also receives up to 50,000 sampled faces per boundary with condition type and prescribed values. Training uses either SI units or per-case scales. ISLA is given all four boundary meshes with condition types and prescribed values, computes per-case scales inside the model, has 67.5 million parameters, and uses four seeds in each of two rows that differ only in training targets. Table 3 prints the float32 rows with cosine decay throughout.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Official scripts**：The paper states that the scoring code will be added to https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo. AeroZoo is not yet present in that repository, so the official scoring scripts described in the paper have not yet been released.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Comparability note**：The scores below are the authors’ own paper-reported runs, not third-party reproductions. In Table 3, GeoTransolver rows are single-seed runs and ISLA rows are means over four seeds; later held-out GeoTransolver figures are means over three or six seeds and should not be mixed with the single-seed Table 3 numbers. The free-stream reference’s median velocity error varies widely across enclosures, so comparisons should use the error ratio and per-enclosure medians.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17152) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17152.pdf) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/AeroZoo) [4](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo) (accessed 2026-11-14)

**Results snapshot**：All figures are paper-reported. On the random split full in per-case scales, the free-stream reference has a median velocity error of 11.0% of the inlet speed. In single-seed runs, GeoTransolver without boundary tokens has a median velocity error of 76.9%, which falls to 8.4% with boundary-condition tokens, beating the reference on 82% of test samples. ISLA, over four seeds, reaches a median velocity error of 5.3%, a near-field velocity error of 9.1%, and a pressure error of 2.3%, beating the reference on 98% of samples at a median ratio of 0.50; trained also on boundary targets, its median velocity error is 5.2%.
