# PRBench – Finance

> In-depth verification: 2026-11-08. The access date is not the publication date or the experiment run date; unknowns are retained, and "Not disclosed" is not inferred.

The PRBench finance track is not a multiple-choice finance exam. It checks real professional advice item by item against an expert rubric. Its 600 public dialogues and the 300 Hard questions stand in an inclusion relation. The law track has another 500 questions, and the same question must not be counted again because of multiple configurations. The current Scale web leaderboard and the Hard scores from the period of the paper are on separate tracks. Where the web page does not state the Full/Hard scope, that scope is not inferred backward from the scores.

## Design goals

### 目标能力

Professional open-ended financial reasoning: financial accuracy, audit transparency, handling of uncertainty, risk and compliance disclosure, and actionability.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 相对既有评测的缺口

It moves from academic questions to questions with economic consequences in real client or professional work, and does not replace professional judgment with a single short answer.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Construction

### 题目与标签来源

The full set has 182 experts in law and finance writing questions from actual work or from client problems; about 30% build the background step by step over at most 10 turns of dialogue.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 出题/标注背景

Finance item writers must hold a master's degree, the CFA, or at least 6 years of professional experience; 182 is the total number of experts in all of PRBench and is not claimed as exclusive to finance.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Review process

Experts from another field review the questions and the rubric, automatic checks cover specifications such as atomicity and self-containment, and independent experts verify them.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Quality control

Full-set rubric-verification agreement is 93.9%; each question contains positively and negatively weighted criteria.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 去污染与隔离

The paper publishes 1,100 conversations and also keeps a private held-out set to monitor contamination of future models; the size of the private set is Unverified. The public data contain a canary field, which is not enough to treat exclusion from the training corpus as completed.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-08）；[3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScaleAI/PRBench/raw/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）

## Data format

### Size

Finance has 600 questions, with Hard at 300; full PRBench has 1,100 questions, including 500 in law. Finance has 10,264 rubric criteria.

Single- and multi-turn English open questions, with the answer on the final turn evaluated by an expert rubric; 13 finance topics.

## Evaluation

### Metrics

A weighted-rubric average; Hard and Full are kept on separate tracks; analysis by category also uses a min-normalized score.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/prbench-finance)（访问 2026-11-07）；[2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-07）；[3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScaleAI/PRBench)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 判分

o4-mini assigns a binary satisfaction label to each rubric item; the weights are the nonzero values from −10 to +10. For each question, the sum of the weighted satisfaction values is first divided by the sum of the positive weights (a single question may score below zero), the mean is then taken across questions, and finally max(0, mean) is applied. Negative scores are not clipped to 0 on each question before the average. Category charts use min-normalized values separately, and they are not mixed with the main metric.

[1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scaleapi/PRBench/main/evals.py)（访问 2026-11-08）；[3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scaleapi/PRBench/main/util.py)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 提示/推理设置

The paper evaluates each model independently 3 times and reports the mean and the 95% confidence interval. GPT-5/O3/Grok-4 are set to High. Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Opus 4.1 have thinking budgets of 32K/16K. Gemini 2.5 Pro uses dynamic. The timeout is 60 minutes, with at most 5 attempts, and the last attempt is lowered to low. Tool experiments are listed separately and are not mixed with results that use no tools.

[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.11562)（访问 2026-11-08）；[3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScaleAI/PRBench/raw/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 官方实现

https://github.com/scaleapi/PRBench. The command is python evals.py config.yaml. results/ reports mean_clipped, and outputs/ keeps the score of each question.

[1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scaleapi/PRBench/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 可比性边界

The current web page's Performance Comparison does not explicitly label Full or Hard. The displayed scores are saved as shown, and the scope is not inferred backward from the values. The paper's Hard scores on the 0–1 scale are a separate track. Rank(UB) is 1 plus the number of models whose confidence-interval lower bound is above this model's upper bound. The HF card's 18,692 rubric criteria conflict with 19,356 in the paper and the README, and they cannot be silently unified. The Full/Hard range of the current leaderboard is Unverified.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/prbench-finance)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScaleAI/PRBench/raw/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）；[3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scaleapi/PRBench/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）
