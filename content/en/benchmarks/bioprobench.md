# BioProBench

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review: 2026-11-08. Supplemented from evidence; items that are not confirmed remain Unverified, and historical scores from papers are recorded separately from the live leaderboard.

What BioProBench examines is procedural understanding of experimental protocols, not biological knowledge question answering that relies only on recalling terminology. Five categories of task separately diagnose question answering, ordering, error correction, generation, and reasoning. Generation among them is closer to a constrained reconstruction of facts already in a protocol, and it cannot be equated with autonomously inventing a new experiment. 523,784 structured instances are the scale of the total resource, and the publicly licensed portion is 350,565. Legal terms and the public scope must be interpreted together with the technical scores.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07889v4) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Construction

### 题源

The corpus is built from human-written public protocols, through cleaning, structuring, and constrained task construction, combined with automatic filtering and expert verification.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07889v4) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 构建人员

The test set is reviewed by experts with doctorates in biology; task facts, steps, and answers come from existing human-written protocols, and the LLM only generates distractors or format conversions under constraint.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）

### 审核

The paper states that every test instance was reviewed by human experts, who checked accuracy, removed potential artifacts, and confirmed relevance.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-07）

### 质控

Quality control that combines automated filtering with expert verification.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-07）

### 去污染及边界

The original protocols are cleaned and deduplicated first; Appendix G tests the sensitivity of PQA to numerical perturbation, and the authors explicitly acknowledge that this cannot rule out memorization of ORD/GEN pretraining sequences. A held-out split cannot be equated with an absence of contamination in pretraining.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）

## Data format

### 规模与计数口径

22,413 original protocols, forming 523,784 structured instances; §3.2 of the body text states explicitly that 350,565 of them come from sources under a license that permits public release and are released, and 523,784 cannot be described as all available for public download.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/YuyangSunshine/bioprobench)（访问2026-11-08）

### Question types

PQA protocol question answering, ORD step ordering, ERR error correction, GEN protocol generation, and REA protocol reasoning.

[来源1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YuyangSunshine/bioprobench/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-07）

### Modalities

Text experimental protocols and structured JSON; the paper explicitly states that image, video, and other multimodal context from real experiments is not currently covered.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）

### Languages

English (official HF metadata language: en).

[来源1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BioProBench/BioProBench/blob/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### Difficulty

Appendix C.2: GEN is stratified as easy, standard, and difficult. The authors set an approximate sampling ratio of 5:2:1, which is not an exact statistic of the full bank. PQA is balanced across reagents, parameters, and operations. ERR is balanced across true and false. ORD distinguishes a main step from a substep.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）

### 划分与公开范围

The official release provides *_train.json and *_test.json for each task; section §3.2 publishes only the 350,565 instances that the license permits, and restricted sources such as JoVE and the Morimoto Lab are excluded from public evaluation sampling. The exact Train and Test counts for each task are Unverified.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/YuyangSunshine/bioprobench)（访问2026-11-08）

## Evaluation

### Metrics

PQA: Accuracy/Brier. ORD: Exact Match/Kendall tau. ERR: Accuracy/Precision/Recall/F1. GEN: BLEU, and keyword and step recall/precision. REA: Accuracy/Precision/Recall/Consistency.

[来源1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YuyangSunshine/bioprobench/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-07）

### 评分

Question-answer accuracy and Brier score, ranking Exact Match and Kendall Tau, error-correction F1, recall of generated steps, and others; these must be reported separately by task.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07889v4) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 提示与运行条件

Appendix C.4 gives a prompt template for each task, and answers are extracted by the specified markers and format. GEN is further split into direct, zero-shot CoT, and one-shot CoT. REA-GEN requires structured reasoning. Different prompts must not be merged into one ranking.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889)（访问2026-11-08）

### 官方评测入口

The official Metrics/ directory provides PQA.py, ORD.py, ERR.py, GEN.py, and REA-ERR.py by task.

[来源1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YuyangSunshine/bioprobench/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-07）

### 可比性边界

Different data versions, subsets, prompts, tool permissions, and model snapshots must not be mixed in a direct comparison; scores in historical papers do not represent the current SOTA.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.07889) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07889v4) (accessed 2026-11-07)
