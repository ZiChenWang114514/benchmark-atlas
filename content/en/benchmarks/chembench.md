# ChemBench

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review: 2026-11-08. Supplemented from evidence; items that are not confirmed remain Unverified, and historical scores from papers are recorded separately from the live leaderboard.

ChemBench places chemistry knowledge, calculation, reasoning, intuition, and safety questions in one reproducible framework. The question sources include both human curation and semi-programmatic generation, and the full set of questions cannot be advertised as written from scratch by experts. It scores by a verifiable final answer, and it does not prove that a model can safely conduct open-ended chemistry research. The real results data on the official site were restored. Verified entries and unverified community submissions are presented separately, so that a high-scoring proxy submission does not obscure a credible comparison.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01475) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://chembench.lamalab.org/) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Design goals

### 测量对象

Measures chemical knowledge, reasoning, confidence calibration, and safety-related capability, and compares them with chemistry experts.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01475) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://chembench.lamalab.org/) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 既有评测的缺口

The chemical expertise of general-purpose models lacks systematic evaluation; knowledge, specialized reasoning, human preference, and safety judgment need to be distinguished.

[来源1](https://chembench.lamalab.org/)（访问2026-11-07）

## Construction

### 题源

Examinations, exercises, the IChO, textbooks, web pages, lecture notes, and questions generated in a semi-programmatic way; Table 1 of v2 states explicitly that 1,749 questions were generated semi-automatically, and all 2,788 questions cannot be described as originally written by a person, question by question. Every question is rewritten, annotated, and reviewed before it is entered into the bank.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475)（访问2026-11-08）

### 构建人员

Built by experts in chemistry and in AI; the backgrounds of the reviewers on the PR include chemistry, materials science, chemical engineering, and physics, and they solve the questions themselves and check the answers by retrieval.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 审核

Questions enter one by one as GitHub pull requests and are merged after experts solve them by hand and retrieval checks cover originality, ambiguity, factual error, topical relevance, and duplication; the review history is kept public.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475)（访问2026-11-08）

### 质控

Experts review each question; CI automatically checks the schema, LaTeX, and format; question-answer hash uuids are used for deduplication; the leaderboard requires evaluation on the full corpus so that selective reporting is avoided.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 去污染及边界

The BigBench canary is carried over, and developers are required to filter the training corpus; the evaluation card states explicitly that “not trained on this corpus” is an expectation rather than a fact that has already been tested. Questions that are public carry a risk of contamination, and the canary, or a search done by hand, cannot be treated as an absolute absence of leakage.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### Question types

Question answering that covers several chemistry topics; it includes knowledge-intensive items, specialized reasoning, and judgments of human preference, and the total number of questions does not stand in for a description of the question types.

[来源1](https://chembench.lamalab.org/)（访问2026-11-07）

### Modalities

The corpus of the original paper is text question answering in chemistry and chemical semantic strings; support for vision-language models in the current engine does not mean that all 2788 of the original questions are multimodal. The multimodal extension is the separate paper arXiv:2411.16955.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench)（访问2026-11-08）

### Difficulty

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Equal-weight fraction correct over all questions; an MCQ is judged fully correct when Hamming loss=0; the default error threshold for a numerical question is 1% of the target value; refusals and confidence are also recorded, and no partial credit is given.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 评分

Prompting follows the model type, and extraction uses regular expressions; instruction models extract the ANSWER span, and if that fails the LLM under test may assist extraction; numeric answers use a 1% threshold, MCQ is scored only when entirely correct, and refusal detection and the retry budget should be recorded separately.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 提示与运行条件

completion and instruction-tuned use different templates, and they handle LaTeX, chemical symbols, units, and special molecular markers. The evaluation card does not impose a uniform limit on temperature, decoding, or compute. Submissions must include the model and data versions.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 官方评测入口

The official Python interfaces are ChemBenchmark.from_huggingface, PrompterBuilder, benchmark.bench, and save_topic_reports; the evaluation card lists compatible engine v0.3.0, which cannot be taken as the data version.

[来源1](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 可比性边界

Different data versions, subsets, prompts, tool permissions, and model snapshots must not be mixed in a direct comparison; scores in historical papers do not represent the current SOTA.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01475) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.01475) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://chembench.lamalab.org/) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 风险与冲突

The HF card carries both the wording “MIT license” and the wording “training prohibited.” The evaluation card says open-science tasks are not evaluated, while the HF publicity says open-ended. What is actually used is a verifiable reference answer, rather than unbounded scoring of open research. Official results mix verified and unverified community submissions, and verified=false cannot be called an official retest.

[来源1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jablonkagroup/ChemBench/blob/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/lamalab-org/chembench/blob/main/eval-card.md)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源3](https://datasets-server.huggingface.co/rows?dataset=jablonkagroup%2FChemBench-Results&config=default&split=2.0.2&offset=0&length=100)（访问2026-11-08）
