# WeatherBench 2

> In-depth review: 2026-11-08. The collection date is not the leaderboard update date; new evidence that has not been obtained does not overwrite an older source date.

WeatherBench 2 compares global numerical weather forecasts on many dimensions of quality, rather than picking one champion that wins every comparison. The original paper of 2020, the scorecard on the official site in 2022, and the year in which each model was trained are kept apart, and a number that an official figure states outright is transcribed as that number. A score is not estimated from color.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [4](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (accessed 2026-11-07).

## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Updates the global medium-range weather-forecast evaluation, comparing data-driven and traditional numerical forecasts.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [4](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Design background

Relative to WB1, it raises the resolution of the data and accessibility in the cloud, provides scoring that can be scaled, adds metrics such as probabilistic forecasts, spectra, and bias, and lets operational physics models verify their own analysis in order to improve the comparability of initial values and of ground truth.

Sources: [1](https://sites.research.google/gr/weatherbench/faq/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

## Construction

### 题目与数据来源

Integrates public training data, ground truth, and baselines, and designs the evaluation according to the practice of operational meteorological centers.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [4](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 构建者背景

Machine-learning researchers and operational numerical-weather researchers at Google Research/DeepMind and ECMWF designed the data and the evaluation; these are not language examination questions written by hand.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### Review process

Grounded in routine verification at meteorological centers and at the WMO. Initial time, verification year, variable, grid, and metric are unified. Operational models use IFS analysis, and models trained on ERA5 use ERA5. The training year and the verification year of each specific model are disclosed for that model. They are not one training set shared by every model.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://sites.research.google/gr/weatherbench/faq/) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### Quality control

The data use first-order conservative regridding, weighted by the overlapping area of grid cells. The default evaluation in the paper is 1.5° (240×121). Resolution, initial time, analysis compared with ERA5, and the limits of reanalysis as ground truth are studied separately. The precipitation example must not be read too strongly as the accuracy of a forecast against observations.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data-guide.html) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [3](https://sites.research.google/gr/weatherbench/faq/) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 去污染与泄漏防范

A weather time series is not a task of removing contamination from text. The initial paper evaluates initializations at 00/12 UTC in 2020, and individual models keep different years in training; the current headline on the official site is 2022. Whether the training years include the test year or future years must be disclosed. WeatherMesh 4 on the official site is marked explicitly as trained on future years, and that does not mean every model follows strict extrapolation in time.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [3](https://sites.research.google/gr/weatherbench/faq/) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

## Data format

### Size

The current data guide: ERA5 material for 1959–2023, a commonly used version at 6 hours and 13 levels, and also original high-resolution files at 1 hour and 37 levels; grids of 1440×721, 240×121 (including both poles), and 64×32 (excluding both poles) are provided. This is not a fixed number of natural-language samples.

Sources: [1](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data-guide.html) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 任务形式

Weather spatiotemporal fields; the paper describes 1–14 days, and the current official-site overview says 1–15 days; the version difference should be retained.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [4](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Modalities

Numerical spatiotemporal meteorological fields: upper-air and surface variables, pressure levels, latitude-longitude grids, and forecast lead times; cloud-optimized Zarr, processed with xarray/Beam. These are not figure-and-text multiple-choice questions.

Sources: [1](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data-guide.html) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-research/weatherbench2/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### Languages

Not applicable: arrays with physical coordinates and units; English variable names and documentation are not a language for evaluating natural-language ability.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data-guide.html) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 难度与类型

Stratified by variable, pressure level, geographic region, forecast lead time, and deterministic or probabilistic task, with no scheme of one difficulty grade per question; the medium range in the original paper is 1–14 days, and the current overview on the official site is 1–15 days.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### Public and hidden splits

Ground truth and baselines are public in the cloud; there is no unified “question-bank train/test.” In the original paper, every initialization in 2020 is at 00/12 UTC, and forecasts may extend into 2021; the current headline on the official site evaluates 2022, and the interactive page can select 2018, 2020, or 2022. The training year of each model must be checked on its own.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [3](https://researchsites.withgoogle.com/weatherbench/deterministic/_dash-layout) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Deterministic forecasts: global RMSE; probabilistic forecasts: CRPS; precipitation additionally uses SEEPS. Comparisons are separate by variable, lead time, and surface or upper air, with no single total score.

Sources: [1](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 评分

Deterministic RMSE, probabilistic-forecast CRPS, and similar metrics are presented separately; there is no single overall leaderboard detached from variable and lead time.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [4](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 提示和复现设置

A prompt does not apply. The initial time, the forecast-start year, lead_time, variable and level, region, and grid are fixed. Operational mode is scored against analysis, and the other modes against ERA5. The deterministic ensemble mean, the single member, and probabilistic CRPS must be kept separate. The current interactive page defaults to Temperature/RMSE/850hPa/Global/2022/240×121.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [3](https://researchsites.withgoogle.com/weatherbench/deterministic/_dash-layout) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 官方脚本

https://github.com/google-research/weatherbench2

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [4](https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 可比性提示

The scorecard on the official site uses the evaluation period of 2022. An operational model is scored against IFS analysis, and the other models against ERA5. The order of the models reflects the date of publication, not a rank. WeatherMesh 4 carries a note that training included future years, and that note needs to be checked against the FAQ.

Sources: [1](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 饱和程度

The official FAQ explicitly declines to name a single “best model,” and saturation cannot be inferred from a high score on a single RMSE/CRPS. Sharpness of the forecast field, bias, calibration of probabilities, extreme events, and regional differences need to be examined together; there is no overall accuracy as a percentage.

Sources: [1](https://sites.research.google/gr/weatherbench/faq/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### 污染、冲突与限制

The official site's headline evaluates 2022, while some model descriptions in the FAQ still follow 2020. The WeatherMesh4 entry writes training on 1971–2019 and 2021–2023 and evaluation on 2020, and that specific context must be retained. ERA5/analysis is not direct station observation. Precipitation in particular should not be treated as a reliable observed ground truth. The original paper's 1–14 days and the official site's 1–15 days are also kept separate.

Sources: [1](https://sites.research.google/gr/weatherbench/faq/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [2](https://sites.research.google/weatherbench/) (accessed 2026-11-08  ) ; [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15560) (accessed 2026-11-08 ).

### Coverage note

The original 2022 upper-level deterministic scorecard PNG on the official site was opened directly, and 11 methods × 4 metrics × 5 lead times = 220 explicitly displayed values were transcribed cell by cell. Values are not estimated from colors or curves. Only models visible in that figure are included. It does not contain every participant, the surface leaderboard, or the probabilistic leaderboard. Operational models and operational-initialization models are scored against analysis, and the rest against ERA5. No single overall ranking is set. The Z500 figure labels are inconsistent with the physical units in the paper. Units are explained by the paper's definitions, and the values are not changed. The FAQ's statement of a 2020 training year cannot be applied automatically to the 2022 figure.
