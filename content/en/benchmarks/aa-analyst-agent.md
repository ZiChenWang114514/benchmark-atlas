## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Using real tables and documents, carry out end-to-end quantitative analysis as a business or data analyst would, including interpreting sources, judging exceptions, and choosing methods.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）

### Design background

Analyst work requires more than arithmetic correctness: the right sources must be chosen, exceptions explained, and the answer reached stably on repetition. How far the work can be entrusted is measured by being fully correct on all 5 runs rather than by an occasional success.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）

### Review process

Reference answers are written by humans and independently verified by Artificial Analysis; the reference answers are not given to the answering agent.

> The official disclosure covers only reference-answer verification; a double-blind, question-by-question review by external experts is not claimed from that.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）

### Quality control

5 independent runs per question; a numeric-equivalence precheck can cover only answers that are certainly correct, and other cases keep the LLM judge's decision. Format, units, and the required precision are all included in scoring.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）

## Evaluation

### Metrics

The primary metric is pass^5 (the proportion correct on all 5 attempts), and pass@1 and pass@5 are also reported

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）

### 评分

Each attempt is scored as pass or fail: every answer is given to a Gemini 3 Flash (Reasoning) judge; a deterministic pre-check of numeric equivalence may override an answer that meets the unit and the precision to a pass, and it cannot override an answer to a failure. pass^5 is the proportion of the 80 questions that are correct on all five attempts; pass@1 is the average accuracy of every attempt; pass@5 is the proportion of questions that are correct at least once.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）

### 运行设置

Stirrup. Each question is run independently 5 times, with at most 100 turns each time. The environment is isolated Linux with Python3.12. It provides the question source files, code execution, URL fetching, image viewing for vision models, and submission of the final answer. Only a number or a label is submitted, with no explanation attached.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-analyst-agent)（访问2026-11-07）
