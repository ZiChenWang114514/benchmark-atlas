# SciUniverse Level 1

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review 2026-11-08.

SciUniverse moves the start of research evaluation forward to the sample, the instrument, and the experimental operation: the model must make a plan and must also face insufficient materials, equipment failure, and measurement noise. Level 1 mixes real experiments, simulated experiments, and interpretation of measured data, and it cannot be summarized as “92 wet experiments.” The overall metric weights 17 families equally, and it is not a simple average of 92 questions.

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Pass@1; equal-weight average across 17 families.

[来源1](https://c5r.net/sciuniverse/)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 评分

Overall Pass@1 gives equal weight to the 17 task families. Within domains, families are equal-weighted as chemistry 7, biology 5, materials 4, and cross-domain 1. Repeated attempts on the same task keep the same task and family weights. Each family has its own success condition. For example, single-reaction optimization uses 3 batches of 8, 24 experiments in total, recommends an already tested condition at the end, and scores the mean yield of an independent confirmation experiment. It does not look only at the highest value during exploration. The pass threshold for single-reaction optimization is a 90% normalized improvement on the gap from the random-search baseline to the constrained oracle, not a raw yield of 90%.

[Source 1](https://c5r.net/sciuniverse/) (accessed 2026-11-08)；[来源2](https://c5r.net/sciuniverse/suites/optimize-one-reaction/)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 饱和与能力边界

The highest overall score currently public is 45.3%, and the six configurations run from 9.4% to 45.3%; an overall full score is not yet shown. This is only a snapshot of one facility’s harness, and a year of saturation is not predicted from it.

[Source 1](https://c5r.net/sciuniverse/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 风险与争议

Simulated tasks reuse public experimental data, and real tasks and simulated tasks must be interpreted separately; the types of failure listed on the official website include physical constraints, experimental habits, knowledge of the protocol, interpretation of results, and evasion of the task. The overall results do not prove reliability in every field of scientific research.

[Source 1](https://c5r.net/sciuniverse/) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 系列与衍生关系

The current official public display is Level 1; the 17 task families are components of the same facility evaluation and are not split into 17 independent benchmarks.

[Source 1](https://c5r.net/sciuniverse/) (accessed 2026-11-08)
