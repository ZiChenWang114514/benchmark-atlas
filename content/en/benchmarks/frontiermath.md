# FrontierMath

> Benchmark Constitution, research snapshot 2026-11-07. The collection time is not the time a score was actually run, or the leaderboard update time.

FrontierMath Tiers1–4 emphasizes original difficult mathematics and a verifiable final answer, but revisions must be tracked. The current v2 addresses a large number of errors in the questions, and the official CSV already contains a Tier4 configuration with a perfect score; this differs from the low-score period of the original paper, and it does not mean that open mathematics problems have been solved along with it.

## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Evaluates advanced mathematics problems that take experts a relatively long time to solve, and retains final answers that can be verified automatically.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Design background

Middle-school contest sets are gradually scoring high, and it is difficult to distinguish advanced mathematical reasoning from real research-level problem solving.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Construction

### Question sources

Mathematics experts write previously unpublished original questions, covering number theory, analysis, algebraic geometry, category theory, and others; answers can be verified by a program.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 出题者背景

Experts in the relevant fields of mathematics design the problems and the verifiers; a typical problem requires related researchers from several hours to several days.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Review process

Independent experts blind-review questions and solutions, check guessability, and verify programmatic scoring.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Quality control

A controlled answer space and a guessproof design; the official revision note of 2026-06-12 corrects a large number of questions and deletes questions, so the version must be tracked.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 污染控制

Original unpublished questions, private evaluation, and encrypted collaboration reduce leakage; OpenAI once had exclusive access to a subset, and the conflict of interest must be disclosed together with that fact.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Data format

### 题量

v2 has 338 questions in total: Tiers1–3 account for 295 and Tier4 for 43; 12 questions are public (10+2), and the rest are private.

[一手资料1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 任务形式

Hard mathematics problems, submitted as a parameterless Python answer function returning an integer, a SymPy object, or a similar value; not merely a natural-language proof.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Modalities

Text (mathematical symbols expressed as text); not an original image or video recognition task

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Languages

English question text; for formal or mathematical notation, see the modality and the repository format

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 难度与分层

Tiers1–3 and the extremely hard Tier4; different tiers cannot be mixed into the same denominator.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Public and hidden splits

12 questions are published as examples; unless noted otherwise, the official hub uses results from the private set.

[一手资料1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Binary correctness per question: 1 if correct, and 0 if wrong or not submitted.

[一手资料1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 评分

Returned Python objects are checked by a verifier; the runtime limit is 30 seconds for answer and for each Python tool call.

[一手资料1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 提示与运行协议

The current official method sets a hard limit of 1000000 tokens on total input plus output, forces submission of the next step after 660000, and provides stateless Python; the configuration of each model is listed separately.

[一手资料1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Saturation

In the current official CSV, the highest mean_score of Tiers1–3 v2 is 0.9368421052631579 (two configurations tied), and Tier4 v2 has a configuration at 1.0; the original paper's description of “below 2%” therefore can no longer be used for the present state, and this aggregate also cannot be used to infer that every hidden question, or every open mathematics problem, has been solved.

Note: Reading of the editorial snapshot: it covers only that CSV and the corresponding protocol, and the raw scores are not equivalent to the open-problem solve rate.

[一手资料1](https://epoch.ai/data/benchmark_data.zip)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 污染与局限

Official v2 states that 42% of the questions were corrected: Tiers1–3 changed 123, Tier4 changed 12, and 5 and 7 were also deleted; a figure below 2% in the original paper cannot be cited as the state of affairs in 2026.

[一手资料1](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 版本/衍生关系

The official site currently divides FrontierMath into three components: Tiers 1–4, Open Problems, and Erdős. This catalog does not split them in order to increase the count. The Erdős portion states that it includes problems that, as of August 2026, were still unsolved and that are formalized in Lean.
