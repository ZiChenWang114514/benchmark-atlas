# LAB-Bench

> Benchmark Constitution, source-by-source in-depth check: 2026-11-08. The collection date does not represent the experiment date; Unverified is not the same as Not disclosed.

LAB-Bench focuses on biological research workflows: literature, supplementary materials, figures, databases, experimental troubleshooting, sequences, and cloning. The full set of 2457 questions in the original paper differs from the public HF subset; the construction process, funding sources, restriction-enzyme corrections, and exact historical scores by subtask are filled in, and the benchmark is not packaged as a real-time overall leaderboard.

Basis of the reading guide: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://github.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futurehouse/lab-bench) (accessed 2026-11-08).

## Construction

### Question sources

Built from the full text, the supplementary material, and the figures of the scientific literature, from databases, from published experimental protocols, and from DNA/RNA/protein sequences and cloning procedures; for some questions about figures a model first generates a candidate question, which an expert then corrects or rewrites, and the whole cannot be described uniformly as originally written by experts.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### 出题者背景

The authors and contracted biology experts manually produced LitQA2, SuppQA, FigQA, TableQA, ProtocolQA, and CloningScenarios; SeqQA and DbQA are generated mainly by programs. PhD-level biology researchers were also asked to establish a human baseline.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### Review process

Human generation and programmatic generation are separate paths; candidate questions from a model for FigQA and TableQA are corrected or rewritten by experts; the distractor options are checked by hand. The paper also uses open-ended answers to check whether a high multiple-choice score comes from guessing by elimination.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### Quality control

For the different subtasks, the source text, the reference answer, and the distractors are checked separately; the paper points out explicitly that poor distractors can inflate a score, and open-answer verification is also carried out. The repository correction of 2025-02-14 fixes restriction-enzyme problems in Cloning/SeqQA.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08 ) ; [2](https://github.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### 去污染与泄漏控制

A private subset of 20% is retained; an official canary string is inserted into the public data to help filter it out before model training.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Question types

Multiple choice, with 8 major categories and 30 fine-grained subtasks, involving literature, databases, supplementary materials, figures, tables, experimental protocols, sequences, and cloning scenarios.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Modalities

Text and images; the inputs of FigQA and TableQA include a list of PIL images.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Languages

The question text is in English and also contains DNA/RNA/protein sequences, database identifiers, and research charts; this is not a balanced evaluation across natural languages.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08 ) ; [2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futurehouse/lab-bench) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### Difficulty

Tool-dependent tasks and tool-independent tasks both exist. The 41 Cloning Scenarios are human-hard multi-step questions. The authors expect that a well-trained molecular biologist may need more than 10 minutes. All 2457 questions are not called a graduate examination indiscriminately.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### Public and hidden splits

About 80% of the data is public, and 20% is retained as a private test subset to monitor training contamination.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

## Evaluation

### Metrics

accuracy, precision, and coverage, also reported by subtask.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 评分规则

The agent returns one option letter; the evaluator aggregates accuracy/precision/coverage for the full set and for subsets.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 提示、采样与工具设置

The official release provides a zero-shot baseline script; a custom agent is given the question, the candidate answers, and any images required.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 官方脚本

labbench.Evaluator.score_agent; scripts/score_baselines.py and run_baselines.sh are used for reproduction.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 可比性警告

Different data versions, subsets, prompts, tool permissions, and model snapshots must not be mixed in a direct comparison; scores in historical papers do not represent the current SOTA.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-07 ) ; [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-07 ) ; [3](https://github.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 饱和度与证据边界

Results in the original paper show uneven capability across chart and sequence subtasks, so a high TableQA score cannot be used to declare the whole suite saturated; only the authors’ historical baseline was obtained, and the current full live SOTA remains Unverified.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### 污染与已知问题

About 20% is held out in private in order to monitor contamination from training, and a canary is added to the public portion; successful filtering is still not guaranteed for every model. Distractors in multiple choice may allow guessing without a real ability to do the work; some tasks depend on a live external database or on permission to use a tool.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10362) (accessed 2026-11-08 ) ; [2](https://github.com/Future-House/LAB-Bench/blob/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08).
