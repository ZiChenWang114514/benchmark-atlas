# Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE)

> Benchmark Constitution, source-by-source in-depth check: 2026-11-08. The collection date does not represent the experiment date; Unverified is not the same as Not disclosed.

HLE measures expert knowledge and reasoning, across disciplines, on answers that can be verified. It is not another name for the ability to make open-ended scientific discoveries. Results on the original multimodal set of 2500 questions on the official site, the text-only evaluation of 2158 questions at AA, and the new Diamond subset are kept strictly apart, and the duplicate official-site scores in the original dossier were removed.

Basis of the reading guide: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://lastexam.ai) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/hle) (accessed 2026-11-08).

## Design goals

### 测量能力

Across higher-level disciplines such as mathematics, science, and the humanities, it evaluates expert-level knowledge and reasoning with definite answers, and calibration of model confidence.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 与既有基准的差异

Older benchmarks such as MMLU are approaching high scores, and harder closed questions, written by experts and with verifiable answers, are needed; this is not open scientific discovery or a definition of AGI.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

## Construction

### Question sources

Experts around the world submit original questions, or a nontrivial synthesis of public knowledge, including text and images; when a question is written, frontier models are tested first and questions that are easy are screened out.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 出题者背景

Nearly 1000 contributors from more than 500 institutions and 50 countries, mostly professors, researchers, or holders of graduate degrees (as stated in the paper).

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Review process

A model difficulty check comes first, followed by 1 to 3 pieces of first-round expert feedback and approval by the organizers or experts; community correction and audit are accepted afterward.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Quality control

A clear problem statement, a unique answer that can be checked, a detailed solution, and a domain label; after release, questions that are wrong or can be found by a direct search are removed.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 去污染与泄漏控制

The README provides an HLE canary string for filtering future training, and it explicitly requires that the benchmark must not enter a training corpus; the presence of a canary does not guarantee that every model has completed the filtering.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/centerforaisafety/hle/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

## Data format

### 规模与统计口径

Both the official homepage and the README list 2500 questions; the README sample output n=2700 is a historical example and is not taken as the current question count.

Sources: [1](https://lastexam.ai) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/centerforaisafety/hle/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Question types

Multiple-choice questions with at least 5 options, and exact short answers; open-ended subjective essays are not included.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Modalities

Text and text+image

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Languages

English question text; for formal or mathematical notation, see the modality and the repository format

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Difficulty

Graduate or expert level, covering more than 100 topics; not every question requires original research.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### Public and hidden splits

2500 public questions plus a private holdout; AA's current text-only set is 2158 questions (May 2025 revision) and is not the full multimodal set.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/humanitys-last-exam) (accessed 2026-11-07).

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Answer accuracy and RMS calibration error.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 评分规则

The original paper uses o3-mini-2025-01-31 to extract answers, judge equivalence, and extract confidence; equivalent formats and a small numerical tolerance are allowed.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 提示、采样与工具设置

The simple evaluation on the official side defaults temperature to 0. For a reasoning model, a max_completion_tokens below 8192 is not advised. The official account says that the largest token budget the model makes available is used, and that collapse has been observed for some models. A comparison has to record the budget that was actually used.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/centerforaisafety/hle/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 官方脚本

https://github.com/centerforaisafety/hle

Sources: [1](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/hle) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 可比性警告

Full multimodal, text-only, settings that include a search tool, different judges, and different revisions of the data cannot be placed directly on a single leaderboard.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19134v1) (accessed 2026-11-07).

### 饱和度与证据边界

The official website explicitly treats Diamond as the migration path for when the original HLE nears the noise ceiling; the original HLE official website peaks at 38.3%, and the AA text-only subset peaks at 61.4%. A unified saturation threshold cannot be inferred from this across different tracks.

Sources: [1](https://lastexam.ai) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/humanitys-last-exam) (accessed 2026-11-08).

### 污染与已知问题

The v12 appendix gives an expert disagreement rate of 15.4% on the audit sample; the biochemistry and health subset is about 18%. This is the disagreement described by the authors, and it is not the error rate of every label.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://lastexam.ai) (accessed 2026-11-07).
