## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Diagnoses generalist manipulation policies in simulation across five dimensions (generalization, memory, precision, long-horizon execution and open instructions) and tests deployment on three real robot platforms

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing benchmarks rely on simple, short-horizon or skill-narrow tasks and usually evaluate only in simulation or only in the real world

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：The team designed 42 simulation tasks in Isaac Sim on the ARX X5 bimanual platform and 18 real-world tasks across three platforms (ARX X5, Piper and Piper X)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Robotics and embodied AI researchers from 18 academic institutions

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Real-world trials are scored independently by three evaluators under a double-blind protocol; official leaderboard entries must pass hidden-layout verification and release model and reproduction code

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：Cross-GPU simulation reruns show success-rate standard deviations of at most 1.1 points; three repeated real-world rounds show overall success-rate standard deviations of at most 1.3 points; an appeal mechanism is provided

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：Official evaluation adds hidden verification layouts beyond the public ones to limit overfitting and leaderboard gaming

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：42 simulation tasks (50 episodes each, 2,100 episodes in total) and 18 real-world tasks (10 trials each); 3,500 simulation and 1,800 real-world training trajectories

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Robot manipulation tasks scored by success rate and step-wise score

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：RGB images, robot states and language instructions

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：English (language instructions)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：On the leaderboard frozen for the paper, the best policy averages an 8.80% simulation success rate against 76.03% for expert human teleoperators; the Open dimension is near zero

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：Public layouts form the main leaderboard; official evaluation adds hidden verification layouts

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Success rate and average score (including partial progress)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Scoring**：The overall result averages the five capability dimensions so that dimensions with more tasks do not dominate; real-world scores average three evaluators

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Prompt settings**：Not applicable (policy evaluation); agent-style systems are listed on a separate agent board and are not ranked on the main board

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Official scripts**：https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Comparability note**：The official board separates agent-style methods from VLA policies; the RoboDojo Sim score on the Claude Fable 5.2 release page comes from internal runs by Anthropic and is not directly comparable with the verified board

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04434) [2](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) [3](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/leaderboard) (accessed 2026-11-10)
