# BEAKER

Uses 1,000 expert-authored QA items to evaluate multimodal models on actionability, knowledge, experimentation, and temporal reasoning for self-driving chemical laboratories.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：In an offline single-turn setting, evaluates multimodal models as embodied brains for self-driving chemical laboratories across actionability, knowledge, experimentation, and temporal reasoning.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing laboratory benchmarks mainly cover safety, anomaly detection, or general scientific understanding, and leave execution-oriented embodied capabilities underexplored.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Images and videos from public chemical-laboratory sources were screened, then human chemistry and embodied AI experts authored all questions and annotations without AI-assisted generation.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：An annotation team of 10 chemistry experts and 12 embodied experts, with senior embodied experts coordinating quality control.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Cross-group blind review assessed scientific correctness, embodied relevance, difficulty, and answer unambiguity; adjudication retained 1,000 items.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：About 1,200 expert-hours; retained spatial annotations show high cross-group agreement.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：The paper discusses possible pretraining exposure of public visual sources and notes that BEAKER questions and answers were newly written.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：1,000 QA items across 11 image and video task types; about 600 image and 400 video items.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Coordinate affordance and trajectory items, multiple-choice questions, and fill-in-the-blank items in an offline single-turn setting.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：Real chemical-laboratory images and short, mid-length, and long videos.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：The paper is in English.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：Across 28 models, overall scores remain far below the human expert score of 93.3; actionability, especially operable-region localization, is a major bottleneck.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：Code and dataset are public on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Task-specific metrics are aggregated into Actionability, Knowledge, Experimentation, and Reasoning, then into an Overall score with weights 0.25 / 0.15 / 0.35 / 0.25.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Scoring**：Accuracy for MCQs; IoU for affordance; a discrete Fréchet-distance-based score for trajectories; expert semantic matching for long-video blanks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Prompt settings**：Unified single-turn prompts and output formats across 28 general-purpose, science-oriented, chemistry-specialized, and embodied multimodal models.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Official scripts**：https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Comparability note**：Scores are author-reported; offline evaluation should not be read as real-robot execution success.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911) [2](https://github.com/HydroSophy/BEAKER) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HydroSophyTech/BEAKER) (accessed 2026-11-10)
