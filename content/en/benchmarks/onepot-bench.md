# onepot-Bench 0

Benchmarks synthetic-chemistry skills for wet-lab execution via ChemAbacus, SynthRefusal, and SynthBench built on private lab data; models are much weaker on SynthBench than on chemistry numeracy items.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Measures tool-free cheminformatics literacy and numerical reasoning (ChemAbacus), synthesis-request refusal and safety behavior (SynthRefusal), and reaction-outcome plus catalyst preference on private wet-lab data (SynthBench).

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：Prior chemistry evaluations often use public data that may appear in training corpora and emphasize knowledge over experimental judgment; lab-aware non-public evaluations are scarce.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：ChemAbacus uses single-molecule SMILES questions; SynthRefusal spans benign to controlled and designer-drug targets; SynthBench uses private experimental data from the company's POT-2 wet lab.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Authors are from Onepot AI, which builds a small-molecule fab and uses language models in its workflows.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Pending verification

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：SynthBench uses never-published internal experimental data to reduce leakage risk.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：SynthBench reactions come from internal wet-lab experiments and are not in a public corpus.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：ChemAbacus 800 questions; SynthRefusal 483 synthesis targets; SynthBench 735 reactions and matched pairs. Thirteen models from six providers are evaluated.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Tool-free chemistry QA, synthesis-request refusal evaluation, and reaction-success plus catalyst-preference judgments.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：SMILES, common names, CAS, InChI, and text prompts.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：Paper and blog are primarily in English.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：Frontier models do relatively well on basic molecular-property questions; on SynthBench, reaction-outcome prediction trails a simple empirical baseline and catalyst preference is not significantly above chance.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：The blog says results are browsable on a benchmarks page; fully public data and scripts are pending verification, and SynthBench data is proprietary.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：ChemAbacus accuracy; SynthRefusal refusal rates and alignment indices; SynthBench reaction-outcome and catalyst-preference performance.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Scoring**：Scores are reported per component; safety behavior varies with molecular representation and target class.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Prompt settings**：Some models use low, medium, and high reasoning effort; DeepSeek V4 Pro and Kimi K2.6 skip SynthRefusal, and Muse Spark skips SynthBench.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Official scripts**：The blog describes an easy-to-run suite; a public evaluation-script link is pending verification.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Comparability note**：Results come from the authors' paper and blog, not independent third-party reruns; SynthBench data is not public, so external reproduction is limited.

Sources: [1](https://www.onepot.ai/blog/onepot-bench-0) [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02595) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02595) (accessed 2026-11-10)
