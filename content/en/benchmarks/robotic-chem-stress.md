# Robotic Chem Stress

Stress-tests LLM agents in a robotic chemistry laboratory: only about 3.3% of 4,608 trials yielded expert-assessed executable workflows, and the best system reached about 28.1%.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Measures physical executability of scientific agents in a robotic chemistry laboratory and whether experimental feedback drives workflow-level replanning or analytical-method redesign.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23045) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.23045) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：Prior evaluations emphasize knowledge, reasoning, and plan generation; scientific agency needs reliable physical action and evidence-driven adaptation, which lack a measurable physical-world testbed.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23045) [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.23045) (accessed 2026-11-10)
