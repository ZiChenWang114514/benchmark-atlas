## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether genomic models can predict the effects of DNA changes and use those predictions to guide sequence design, across five capabilities: sequence priors, functional prediction, biological context, genetic interactions and genomic design

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing benchmarks mostly measure aggregate predictive accuracy and cannot show whether models separate cell-type-specific effects, capture interactions between mutations, or help select candidates that reach a target response

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Eight tasks built on five experimental datasets, including OpenSplice splicing mutations, COMPASS double mutations, Siraj measurements across cell types and GTEx tissue expression

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Machine learning and life science researchers at the University of Copenhagen and partner institutions

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：Evaluation splits grouped by gene, regulatory element, locus or sequence family; experimental references built from repeat measurements; block bootstrap over biological units for confidence intervals

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：The paper notes that holding out data does not imply the sequences were absent from foundation-model pretraining

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：Eight tasks on five experimental datasets; for example OpenSplice covers 590,104 mutations in 466 genes

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Numerical evaluations of sequence-prior loss, mutation-effect correlation, tissue identification, interaction prediction and candidate selection

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：DNA sequences and experimental measurements

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：Not applicable (DNA sequences)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：The paper states genomic models have yet to saturate even simple tasks; mean utility on the multi-cell design task remains negative

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：All code and fixed data splits are released; data are hosted on Hugging Face

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Bits-per-base loss, within-group Spearman correlation (macro-averaged), tissue identification accuracy, first-choice edit percentile, cell-selectivity utility and multi-cell gain

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Scoring**：Most tasks fit linear readout heads on frozen embeddings, with native outputs reported separately; correlations are computed within each gene or element and averaged with equal weight

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Prompt settings**：Not applicable (not a prompt-based evaluation)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Official scripts**：https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Comparability note**：Linear-head results may depend on each model's embedding size and input context length; experimental references are fairly naive and are not performance ceilings

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806) [2](https://github.com/machinelearninglifescience/BEND2) [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yhsure/BEND2) (accessed 2026-11-10)
