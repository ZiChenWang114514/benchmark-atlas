# GEO-Bench

> Benchmark Constitution, check date: 2026-11-07. Unknowns remain Unverified; the access date is not presented as the experiment date.

GEO-Bench is a 12-task transfer benchmark for pretrained vision models in Earth monitoring, not a remote-sensing question-answering set for an LLM to answer. Six classification tasks and six segmentation tasks emphasize few-shot settings, sensor diversity, and statistical comparability across tasks; original version 1.0 and the officially recommended GEO-Bench-2 should keep a clear version boundary.

Sources for the reading guide: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03831v2/](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03831v2/) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Design goals

### 评测能力

Compares foundation models oriented toward Earth monitoring, and provides an evaluation more closely aligned with remote-sensing applications than general image tasks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03831v2/) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 设计动机

Ordinary natural-image benchmarks leave out the multispectral, SAR, spatial-resolution, and few-label transfer conditions of remote sensing; 12 downstream tasks and the reporting protocol are unified, in order to test whether pretrained representations can adapt to real Earth-monitoring tasks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Construction

### 材料来源

Domain experts compiled and adapted existing Earth-monitoring datasets, covering multiple countries and multiple sensor modalities.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03831v2/) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 编制背景

Six industry experts took part in choosing topics, covering fields such as forestry and climate science; a scientific steering committee is responsible for high-level decisions.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### Review process

Existing data are selected and adapted by ease of use, real-world use, diversity of modality and of task, and a permissive license; detection tasks and counting tasks are converted to semantic segmentation; the original split is kept, or a validation set and a test set with no spatial overlap are rebuilt.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### Quality control

Datasets with too many training samples are downsampled to at most 20,000, approximately balancing the major classes; the conversion pipeline, unified loading format, and statistical-report specification are public.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 去污染边界

Spatial separation is used to reduce geographic leakage across the downstream train, validation, and test splits; it is not the same as proof that every pretraining image is free of overlap with the test regions. An audit of overlap with the pretraining corpus remains Unverified.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### Question types

Image classification, including multi-label, and pixel semantic segmentation, 6 tasks each; the original detection and counting tasks have been converted to segmentation, and this is not text question answering.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### Modalities

Geographic rasters including multispectral, SAR, hyperspectral, elevation, and cloud probability; the sensors include Sentinel-1/2, Landsat-8, and airborne RGB, with a spatial resolution from 0.1 to 30 m/pixel.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### Languages

A language label for text question answering does not apply: the input is numerical remote-sensing imagery and the output is a class or a segmentation mask; the task metadata and the documentation use English.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/geo-bench/HEAD/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### Difficulty

There is no single grade of question difficulty; these are few-shot transfer tasks, and the paper compares the full set and different training-set sizes together. The easy EuroSAT subtask must not stand for all 12 tasks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 公开及隐藏划分

train, validation, and test are released; the original split of the data is kept whenever possible, and otherwise a validation set and a test set are generated with no spatial overlap. Exact counts for each part are in the scale field.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Classification reports accuracy; segmentation reports IoU. Across tasks, values are first normalized linearly by the minimum and maximum of the official baselines, then the IQM is computed and stratified bootstrap confidence intervals are reported; a normalized result may exceed 1.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 计分

The recommendation is at most 16 hyperparameter trials per task, with early stopping on the validation set, and at least 10 random seeds for the chosen configuration; IQM trims the highest and the lowest 25% each, and uncertainty is estimated with 1000 bootstrap samples.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 提示与运行设置

Not a test in the form of a language prompt; whether the encoder is fine-tuned or frozen, the input band mapping, the normalization, the resolution, and the data augmentation should be fixed. The paper recommends rotation by 90 degrees and horizontal and vertical flips.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03831) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/geo-bench/HEAD/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07)
