# MysteryMechanism

> Verification date: 2026-11-08. Unknowns are not filled in; the date on which the source was accessed is not the experiment run date.

MysteryMechanism requires an agent to recover a hidden mathematical law within an experiment budget of 2d+1, starting from anonymized variables, physical boundaries, and two passive observations. The 222 mechanisms cover multiple classes of scientific relation. Validation and test mechanisms stay private, and only three frozen examples are public. A submission is judged by functional equivalence on private probes, and the formula text is not required to match. The public demonstration path is not a verbatim trajectory of the model. Success without stating the source domain also does not prove that prior scientific knowledge was unused.
[Reading basis for this page](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Review process

There is also a blinded post hoc audit of whether successful trajectories explicitly identify the source domain; this is not the per-question expert-review record from construction of the mechanism question bank, and the latter is Unverified.

> 状态：待核实。

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

### Quality control

Expressions are executed on structural probes that are private and sampled continuously, and they are scored as pass or fail according to whether the normalized error is below a noise-aware threshold. A failure of the provider or of the agent is scored zero. A separate blind audit after the fact records whether a successful trajectory explicitly identified the domain. Not having stated the domain is not proof that no prior was used.

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 去污染/泄漏控制

Variables are anonymized, no domain background is given, and there is no network; publicly frozen examples do not expose the verification or test mechanism.

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

## Data format

### 精确规模与统计口径

222 mechanisms, covering biology, physics, chemistry, engineering, ecology, and abstract dynamics.

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

### Question types

Anonymous input variables, a physical range, and two noisy passive observations; after a limited number of experiments, one executable expression may be submitted. The three public paths are condensed demonstrations of success, constructed by hand, not the verbatim trajectory of a run of some model.

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 难度结构

Limits from sparse and noisy observations and costly experiments: a d-dimensional mechanism allows at most 2d+1 additional experiments and is given only 2 passive observations; the public two-dimensional example has 5 additional experiments, which cannot be extrapolated so that every question is fixed at 5.

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 污染与已知争议

The mechanism is given no context of domain and no internet. The mechanisms for validation and for test are private. That does not amount to proof that the model used no prior scientific knowledge. The official site states explicitly that “domain not identified” means only that the successful trajectory did not state the correct source. A path shown in a public example is a demonstration, not a word-for-word output of a model.

[来源1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mysterymechanism)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 衍生与条目关系
