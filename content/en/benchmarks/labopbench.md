# LabOPBench

Uses 756 expert-authored open-ended items to test whether multimodal models can make context-consistent laboratory judgments from real experimental evidence.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether multimodal models can turn laboratory evidence and experimental context into open-ended judgments about experimental state, likely causes, risks, and next operations.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The paper argues that existing science benchmarks rarely assess whether models can convert experimental observations into context-consistent laboratory judgments.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Authored by 22 contributors with hands-on lab experience from real laboratory materials: 664 organic-synthesis and 92 materials-science questions.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Contributors include PhD researchers, postdocs, laboratory specialists, and associate researchers or above; 20 of 22 had at least five years of laboratory experience.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Each item was independently reviewed by two additional experts for scientific validity, evidence sufficiency, and scoring clarity; substantive disagreements were adjudicated by a third expert.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：Question-specific rubrics use 0-3 anchors. An LLM was used only for post hoc linguistic standardization and was not allowed to change scientific content.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：756 open-ended items: organic synthesis 664 (preparation 67, monitoring 30, postprocessing 425, characterization 142) and materials science 92.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Open-ended judgment items with experimental context, multimodal evidence, and a question-specific scoring rubric.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：Laboratory photographs, instrument interfaces, spectra, chromatograms, microscopy images, sample states, or experimental records.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：The paper is in English.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：Eight multimodal models scored 28.8% to 45.8% overall; postprocessing had the lowest cross-model mean at about 34.9%.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：The paper reports full question counts and results; a GitHub repository is public, while dataset licensing details remain to be verified.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Evaluation

**Metrics**：Question-specific 0-3 rubrics normalized to percentages; scores from two judge models (GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro) are averaged.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Scoring**：Open-ended answers are scored by rubric; half points are allowed; explicitly independent subquestions may be split then averaged.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Prompt settings**：Eight models were evaluated on all 756 items under a unified input protocol without reference answers, rubrics, or external tools.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Official scripts**：Unverified

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Comparability note**：Scores are author-reported and use model judges; the authors report close agreement with expert spot checks, but this is not a fully independent third-party retest.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19780) [2](https://github.com/johnnylee00/LabOPBench) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19780) (accessed 2026-11-10)
