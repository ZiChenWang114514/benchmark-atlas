## Design goals

**What it measures**：Reproduce derivations of published theoretical results, and derive consequences when theoretical conditions are modified.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32575) (accessed 2026-11-08)

**Gap in earlier benchmarks**：The paper says competition problems and scientific-programming evaluations do not check the assumptions and completeness of a theoretical-physics derivation, or whether computational evidence supports a general conclusion.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32575) (accessed 2026-11-08)
