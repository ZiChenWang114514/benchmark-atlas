# Remote Labor Index

> In-depth verification: 2026-11-08. The access date is not the publication date or the experiment run date; unknowns are retained, and "Not disclosed" is not inferred.

The Remote Labor Index evaluates end-to-end remote work with real freelance projects and human deliverables. Of the 240 projects, 230 are used for private quantitative evaluation and 10 are public for qualitative inspection. Human review decides whether a deliverable meets the acceptance standard of a reasonable client. Automation Rate is not an economy-wide rate of employment replacement, and the public review platform is not a scorer that can automatically reproduce the private overall leaderboard.

## Design goals

### 目标能力

End-to-end completion of remote freelance projects that have real economic value and are acceptable to a client.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 相对既有评测的缺口

Shifts from isolated skills and research problems to professional deliverables produced by real transactions, compared directly with human standards.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Construction

### 题目与标签来源

550 initial projects were collected from 358 verified freelancers on Upwork, screened down to 240, covering 23 fields.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 出题/标注背景

Experienced freelance professionals.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Review process

Screened by completeness, reproducibility, and professional quality; trained human experts compare the deliverables.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Quality control

Personal information is deleted or replaced with synthetic information; a majority vote of 3 independent reviewers, and the official site reports an Automation Rate agreement of 94.4%.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 去污染与隔离

230 private projects are included in the quantitative evaluation; 10 public projects are for qualitative analysis only.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Automation Rate is the primary metric; Elo (anchored to humans at 1000), Dollars Earned, and Cost Savings are secondary metrics.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 判分

Humans compare AI and human deliverables: 1 is failure, 2 meets the standard, and 3 exceeds it, and 2 or 3 counts as automation success; Elo additionally makes pairwise quality comparisons between models.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 提示/推理设置

A CLI OpenHands or computer-use environment, with one prompt and multimedia tools; the maximum generation budget is 30 US dollars per task; the leaderboard reports the best environment for each agent.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 官方实现

https://github.com/centerforaisafety/rli_evaluation_platform; this is a platform for human comparison of multimodal deliverables, not a scorer that can automatically reproduce an overall leaderboard of 230 questions. The README provides 10 public tasks and sample AI deliverables.

[1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/centerforaisafety/rli_evaluation_platform/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/rli-public-set/raw/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 可比性边界

It measures only the remote projects that were sampled, and it does not represent the rate at which economic labor as a whole can be substituted; physical labor, direct interaction with customers, and long-term acceptance were excluded. The launch figure of 2.5% is a historical result and does not replace the current leaderboard.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Saturation

The current official high Automation Rate is 21.25%, far below 100%, but this is a success rate under a particular project sample and acceptance protocol, not a rate at which labor across the whole economy can be replaced.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 污染/限制/争议

Fully manual review limits high-frequency expansion; public examples carry a contamination risk; acceptance criteria for professional work cannot be equated with multiple-choice accuracy.

[1](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)（访问 2026-11-07）
