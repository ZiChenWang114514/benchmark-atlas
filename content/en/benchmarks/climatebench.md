# ClimateBench

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth verification snapshot 2026-11-07. Unverified is not the same as Not disclosed; the collection date is not the leaderboard update time.

ClimateBench v1.0 tests the simulation from an emissions pathway to a long-term climate response, rather than day-by-day weather forecasting or a prediction of real observations. The official data have published the test scenario SSP245; MIT for the code and CC BY 4.0 for the data must be recorded separately. The main leaderboard penalizes both regional spatial error and global-mean error, and a column for temperature alone cannot replace the ranking across tasks.

Basis of the reading guide: [1](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021MS002954) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Design goals

**测量能力**：Evaluate how well machine learning can simulate the climate response of complex Earth-system models, in support of exploring different emissions pathways.

Sources: [1](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021MS002954) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**针对的不足**：The task is a low-cost simulation of the long-term climate response of NorESM2 under different pathways of emissions and forcing, and it must maintain both the global mean and the regional spatial pattern. It is not a weather forecast, and it is not a reconstruction of real observations.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Construction

**材料来源**：NorESM2 simulation output and the corresponding forcing data; drawn from several CMIP6 experiments, with more than one ensemble member included when the conditions allow.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**出题背景**：Teams spanning climate science, meteorology, statistics, and machine learning; the author-contribution section distinguishes DWP’s data processing, the NorESM2 simulations of DO/ØS, and the development of baseline emulators by the other members.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**审核**：The authors processed CMIP6 simulations and checked them against several classes of baselines; the paper was published in JAMES. The public history of data versions records the CO2 accumulation, the alignment of aerosols and greenhouse gases, and the repair of missing values for the temperature diurnal range.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**质控**：Output changes are computed from the piControl climatology; multiple ensemble members and the 2080–2100 mean are used to reduce internal variability; Zenodo retains the error revisions 0.2, 0.3, and 0.4.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**去污染边界**：Evaluation holds out the full SSP245 scenario. This is scenario generalization, not decontamination of LLM pretraining; other scenarios are used for training, and a random mix of time points cannot be equated with the official protocol.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Data format

**规模与统计口径**：A latitude-longitude grid of 96×144. 4 input forcings (CO2, CH4, SO2, and BC) and 4 outputs (tas, diurnal range, pr, and pr90). The neural-network configuration in the paper uses 754 training samples of 10-year windows, which cannot be treated as the only question count of the whole dataset.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**任务形式**：Spatiotemporal numerical regression: annual temperature, diurnal temperature range, mean precipitation, and the spatial field of 90th-percentile precipitation are predicted from emissions and forcing pathways.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**模态**：Climate spatiotemporal numerical field；Global scalar emissions time series

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**语言**：Numerical-simulation data and Python scientific-computing notebooks, not a natural-language question-answering set.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**难度**：Primarily a held-out SSP245 evaluation; the appendix also has a harder SSP245-aer scenario with aerosol change only, which cannot be ranked directly together with the main leaderboard.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**公开/保留划分**：The official source provides train_val.tar.gz and test.tar.gz; the test scenario SSP245 is now public, and version 0.1 initially held it back for the duration of a hackathon; the data version should not be miswritten as permanently hidden.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Evaluation

**指标**：Spatial, global, and total NRMSE; four outputs under the SSP245 scenario for 2080—2100, namely tas, diurnal temperature range, pr, and pr90; ranked by the mean of total NRMSE across tasks.

Sources: [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**评分**：Averaged over 2080–2100 and over the three target ensemble members; weighted by latitude area, Total NRMSE = Spatial NRMSE + 5 × Global NRMSE; the README ranks by the mean of Total across the four outputs. CRPS is a probability metric reported separately, and it does not enter that headline ranking.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**提示与运行设定**：Not an LLM-prompt evaluation; the forcing input, the training scenario, the historical window, the average over the target set, and the SSP245 test period must be held fixed.

Sources: [1](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-07); [3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7064308) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**脚本/运行入口**：https://github.com/duncanwp/ClimateBench

Sources: [1](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021MS002954) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07)

**可比性**：Different data versions, subsets, prompts, tool permissions, and model snapshots must not be mixed in a direct comparison; scores in historical papers do not represent the current SOTA.

Sources: [1](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021MS002954) (accessed 2026-11-07); [2](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/87693/1/WatsonParris_etal_2022_ClimateBench_AAM.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-07)
