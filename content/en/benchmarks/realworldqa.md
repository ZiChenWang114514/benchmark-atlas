# RealWorldQA

> In-depth verification: 2026-11-08. The access date is not the publication date or the experiment run date; unknowns are retained, and "Not disclosed" is not inferred.

RealWorldQA consists of images of real scenes and of question answering that is easy to verify, and it mainly examines basic spatial understanding; the initial official HF test has 765 rows. The evidence of its original release is the announcement of 2024 and the dataset card. An independent original paper, or an official code repository, has not been confirmed, and a paper about another model is not used as the original document of the benchmark. The vendor comparison at the 2024 first release and the model-judge protocol of the 2026 research are kept strictly separate.

## Data format

### Size

The official HF card has a test split of exactly 765 rows, and each row contains image, question, and answer; num_bytes on the card is 678,377,246. download_size and dataset_size differ because the packaging is accounted for differently, and they are not both called the total size of the images. The announcement says only over 700.

[1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xai-org/RealworldQA/raw/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://x.ai/news/grok-1.5v)（访问 2026-11-08）

### Saturation

The highest score in the original release comparison is 68.7%; Table 2 of the 2026 MMVistaReason paper contains a RealWorldQA result as high as 80.9%, but its model-judge protocol differs from the first release, so this cannot be taken as a saturation trend under one fixed protocol. The author-model score of 77.4% also cannot be mistaken for the highest score in the whole table.

[1](https://x.ai/news/grok-1.5v)（访问 2026-11-08）；[2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01352v2)（访问 2026-11-08）
