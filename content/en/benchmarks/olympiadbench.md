# OlympiadBench

> Benchmark Constitution, verification 2026-11-08. Unknowns remain "Unverified"; an access date is not an experiment date.

OlympiadBench evaluates open-ended scientific reasoning in difficult mathematics and physics, rather than a standalone bank of physics multiple-choice questions. Full modality and text only, and solving and proving, use different evaluation paths; the question counts of the full bank and of the physics subset are distinguished, and the statement that the code is MIT is separated from the statement that the HF data are Apache-2.0.

Source: [Original description](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [Result evidence](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Evaluates high-difficulty reasoning with olympiad-level bilingual multimodal science problems; the science section gives priority to displaying the physics subset.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 设计背景与适用边界

Earlier scientific question answering leaned toward multiple choice, a single language, or lower difficulty; this benchmark places open mathematics and physics questions, from the gaokao through competitions, together with bilingual input and image-text input, in one framework of question types, but text and image-text, and proofs and problem solving, must be evaluated separately.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Construction

### 题目或数据来源

Selected from mathematics and physics contests and from Chinese college-entrance examination questions, with expert-level step-by-step reasoning annotations.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 构建者背景

The author team of the Department of Computer Science and Technology and the Institute for Artificial Intelligence at Tsinghua University, the Institute of Artificial Intelligence at Beihang University, and Wisdom Way AI Lab; they manually proofread and annotated the official contest PDFs, and they do not claim to have originally authored every question anew.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Review process

Mathpix converts the official examination PDFs to Markdown; the team checks each item by hand against the original PDF, then structures it as stem—solution—answer, with the topic and the question type annotated by hand.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Quality control

Mathpix OCR parses the official PDF, which is then checked, cleaned, revised, deduplicated, and labeled with answer type and subdomain.

Sources: [1](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 去污染与泄漏边界

Questions are taken from the official contest PDFs rather than from web sites that aggregate them, and the format is converted in order to reduce the risk of leakage; a small model trained on a corpus of mathematical symbols vectorizes the questions and removes duplicates by cosine similarity. This does not prove that public historical examination questions never entered model training.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Data format

### Size

Paper v2 has 8,476 questions in the full bank: 6,142 in mathematics and 2,334 in physics. 4,869 questions include figures (2,911 in mathematics and 1,958 in physics). These are statistics from the paper. The HF card field size_categories:10M is not mistaken for the question count.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hothan/OlympiadBench/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 题型与交付物

6,728 open-ended solving questions and 1,748 theorem-proving questions; answer types include numerical values, expressions, equations, intervals, and tuples. Proof questions are not included in the regular automatically scored results.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Modalities

- Text/LaTeX
- Figures supplied with the questions (a text-only subset also exists)

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Languages

English and Chinese, distinguished by the filenames en/zh.

Sources: [1](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Difficulty

At the level of mathematics and physics contests and the Chinese college-entrance examination; Table 2 of the paper gives CEE:COMP as 66%:34%, which is not a single difficulty score applied to every question.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Public and hidden splits

The official release publishes questions by category, together with answers, solutions, and figures, and HF is split into 18 configs; experiments use a fixed zero-shot template directly, rather than tuning on a small validation set; an independent hidden test track has not been verified.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hothan/OlympiadBench/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Answers are evaluated by subject; bilingual and multimodal settings must be recorded separately, and the mean of mathematics and physics must not be used as the physics score.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Scoring

After the answers to open questions are standardized, they are scored automatically with eval/auto_scoring_judge.py; proof questions are evaluated on a sample and are not mixed into the same automatic accuracy.

Sources: [1](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 提示与运行设置

The official experiments use zero-shot; separate templates are provided for Chinese and English open questions, and text and multimodal settings are reported separately.

Sources: [1](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 评测脚本

https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 可比性提醒

Different data versions, subsets, prompts, tool permissions, and model snapshots must not be mixed in a direct comparison; scores in historical papers do not represent the current SOTA.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 饱和程度

The historical all-modality table in the README gives GPT-4o at 13.10% in physics and 32.48% in mathematics; this is evidence that the historical experiment still had a gap, and it does not represent the saturation of current frontier models in 2026.

Sources: [1](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 争议与已知问题

Public historical contest questions may have been included in pretraining; automatic symbolic judgment does not understand the specific physical conditions, and discussion of proofs and of text classification needs human evaluation. The README Overview still calls GPT-4V the best, while the results table has already added GPT-4o, and each is recorded from its own table. The HF scale label of 10M is inconsistent with the 8,476 questions in the paper, and that scale label is not accepted.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14008) (accessed 2026-11-08); [2](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench) (accessed 2026-11-08); [3](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hothan/OlympiadBench/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)
