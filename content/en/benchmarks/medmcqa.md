# MedMCQA

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review 2026-11-08. The access date is not the date the experiment was run; dates from historical sources keep their original record.

MedMCQA comes from the AIIMS and NEET PG medical entrance examinations in India, and from practice questions, and it covers 21 medical subjects. The exact scale after cleaning in the original paper is 193,155 questions; the 194k in the abstract and on the official site is the accounting of an overview. In reproduction, the most important risks are not only contamination. They also include disagreement among the original paper, the README, and HF on the dev and test labels, and differences in the answer index.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Design goals

### 测量能力

Multidisciplinary knowledge and reasoning from Indian medical entrance and postgraduate examinations, with explanations to support medical QA training and diagnosis.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 设计动机

Existing medical multiple-choice sets are small in scale and limited in topic; a larger, multidisciplinary benchmark with solutions is needed.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Construction

### 材料来源

A bank of questions in the style of the real AIIMS and NEET PG examinations.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 命题与标注背景

Every author of the paper is affiliated with Saama AI Research; the materials come from official historical examinations for AIIMS/NEET PG and from mock examinations and online question banks written by medical professionals. The identity, question by question, of the people who originally set the questions is not inferred in this entry.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Review process

Section 2.3 of the paper filters out a question that does not have four options, that has no answer or an empty set of candidates, that depends on an image or a table, or that depends on a particular piece of outside information. Grammar and spelling are corrected with Grammarly under the supervision of a person, and duplicates are then removed. A random 25% of the questions is also drawn for an analysis of question type and of reasoning type. That analysis does not claim that every question was given a medical review, question by question, under a double-blind procedure.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Quality control

Paper §2.4 checks similarity between questions with Levenshtein distance, and questions with similarity greater than 0.9 are removed from dev and test; the split follows the examination source rather than assigning questions at random, in order to reduce leakage from duplicate questions.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 去污染与留出

A public question bank and public training materials are easily exposed; deduplication is not the same as excluding LLM pretraining contamination.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Data format

### 规模与统计对象

The exact total after cleaning is 193,155 questions (182,822+6,150+4,183), consistent with §2.3 and Tables 1 and 2 of the paper; the abstract and the official-site publicity still write more than194k, and that publicity approximation cannot be taken as the exact scale. 21 medical subjects and 2.4k topics are the official overview accounting.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 题型与字段

These are four-option medical multiple-choice questions, and cop gives the correct option; choice_type may be marked single or multi. The HF card explains that multi can mean an option contains a combined sub-item, and it does not mean that several letters may be returned at will. The answer index in the original GitHub submission is 1–4, and the HF features class_label is 0–3, so the index must be converted.

Sources: [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Modalities

Text stems, four candidate options, answers, explanations, and subject information.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### Languages

English.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 难度与覆盖

At medical-graduate examination level, mixing knowledge and reasoning; there is no general difficulty banding.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 公开/私有与数据划分

The sources label the splits in opposite directions. The GitHub README table header has Train/Valid/Test as 182,822/6,150/4,183. Paper Table 2, §3, and the actual HF features have Train/Test/Dev = 182,822/6,150/4,183. The end of paper §2.4 also writes about 6k dev and 4k test, which conflicts with its Table 2. Actual use is recorded by the split name and the row count of the downloaded file, and the two are not silently swapped. The official test answers on the original GitHub are not public. The HF card's test6150 and validation4183 must be listed separately.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Accuracy; with context and without context, and dev and test, are noted.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 评分与聚合

Answer options are matched; explanations may be used for training or retrieval, but the reference answer must not be leaked at test time.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 提示与推理预算

The official site separates with/without context; historical settings such as the 2022 Codex5-shot CoT are not placed directly on the same ranking as modern RAG agents.

Sources: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 官方实现

The official train.py is for reproducing the experiments; the hidden test requires a CSV submitted as the README specifies, with columns id and Prediction (correct option), and answer indices 1—4.

Sources: [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 可比性边界

The column on the official web page is labeled Acc(%) but displays values as 0.xx, and the original fraction is kept; test and dev, with and without context, and the HF split and the original GitHub split are kept separate. The original submission index 1–4 and HF0–3 cannot be mixed directly; the 90.0% on the independent AMR subset does not replace the official test result.

Sources: [https://medmcqa.github.io/](https://medmcqa.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa/raw/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19029v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19029v1) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 饱和判断边界

What can be seen on the official site now is historical results from 2022, and AMR reports 90.0% on an independent MedMCQA subset; there is no comparable evidence updated with the same full set, the same split, and the same scorer, so a high score on a subset cannot be used to judge the full set saturated.

Sources: [https://medmcqa.github.io/](https://medmcqa.github.io/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19029v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19029v1) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 污染、冲突与复现风险

A public question bank and public explanations carry a risk of pretraining exposure. Similarity deduplication in the original paper does not prove that modern models are uncontaminated. A more direct reproduction risk is inconsistent dev/test labeling inside the paper and the README, and HF label indices of 0–3 differing from the original submission's 1–4.
