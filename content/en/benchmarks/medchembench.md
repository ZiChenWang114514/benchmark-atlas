# MedChemBench

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review 2026-11-08. The access date is not the date the experiment was run; dates from historical sources keep their original record.

MedChemBench is an internal evaluation used by OpenAI for reasoning in medicinal chemistry, covering workflows such as understanding of structure, structure-activity relationships, and judgments of efficacy and toxicity. The new version stated in the system card of 2026-10-30 is v7. It uses 99 questions, averages the rubric score inside a question first, and then weights questions equally; 49.6% should therefore not be read as 49.6% of the questions passing in full.

Sources: [https://openai.com/index/introducing-new-capabilities-to-gpt-rosalind/](https://openai.com/index/introducing-new-capabilities-to-gpt-rosalind/) (accessed 2026-11-08); [https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-rosalind-6/medicinal-chemistry](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-rosalind-6/medicinal-chemistry) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Evaluation

### Metrics

v7 mean rubric score (%): answer scores are first averaged within each question, then questions are averaged with equal weight; it is not a binary pass rate over questions.

Sources: [https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-rosalind-6/medicinal-chemistry](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-rosalind-6/medicinal-chemistry) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 评分与聚合

Under each reasoning budget in v7, each question has at most 9 answers that are scored; the mean inside the question is taken first, and the questions are then averaged with equal weight; the system card reports the mean rubric scores under the highest reasoning budget that was tested. The full rubric and the implementation of the scorer remain Unverified.

核验状态：待核实

Sources: [https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-rosalind-6/medicinal-chemistry](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-rosalind-6/medicinal-chemistry) (accessed 2026-11-08)

### 提示与推理预算

The two v7 models are compared on the same 99 questions, the same benchmark version, and the same question set, at the highest reasoning budget that was tested; at most 9 answers per question. The exact prompt and token budget are Unverified.
