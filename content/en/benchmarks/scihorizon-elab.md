# SciHorizon-eLab

Compiles natural-language lab protocols into executable embodied tasks and releases SciVLABench with 300 certified tasks; the strongest reported policy averages about 49.7% success.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether scientific embodied agents can carry out laboratory operations from experimental protocols, with human-agent coordination, expert-demonstration replay, and step-level success checks.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：Prior lab simulation benchmarks often rely on manual task engineering, which makes it hard to compile diverse scientific protocols into executable, verifiable embodied tasks at scale.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Natural-language scientific experiment protocols are compiled via semantic grounding, executable task synthesis, and multi-stage simulation-based certification.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Authors are from CNIC, CAS and Beihang University.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Tasks enter SciVLABench after multi-stage simulation certification; the report includes compilation and certification audits.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：The pipeline produces semantically grounded environments, executable manipulation programs, and step-level success specifications, with reproducible expert demonstrations and execution traces.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：Pending verification

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：SciVLABench contains 300 certified laboratory-operation tasks across diverse lab operations.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Embodied manipulation tasks with human-agent coordination and ordered step-level evaluation.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：Natural-language protocols, simulation environments, manipulation programs, and execution traces.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：Paper and repository are primarily in English.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：The strongest reported policy averages about 49.7% success on representative tasks; human-agent coordination evaluations show clear weaknesses.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：Code, benchmark data, and evaluation toolkit are released on GitHub.

Sources: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30971) [2](https://github.com/SciHorizon-elab/SciHorizon-elab) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30971) (accessed 2026-11-10)
