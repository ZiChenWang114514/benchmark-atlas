# MMMU

> Verification date: 2026-11-08. Unknowns are not filled in; the date on which the source was accessed is not the experiment run date.

MMMU uses 11550 university and professional-subject questions with images and text to examine professional knowledge and visual reasoning. dev, validation, and test have 150, 900, and 10500 questions respectively. On 2026-02-12 the official source released the test answers, so this page no longer calls it a hidden-answer test set. Paper v4 writes 30 image types, and the current README writes 32. The two lack an official mapping note. They are retained side by side rather than forcibly unified.
[Guide basis for this page](https://github.com/MMMU-Benchmark/MMMU/blob/main/README.md) (accessed 2026-11-08)

[Additional guide source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502) (accessed 2026-11-08)

## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Perception of images and text, use of knowledge, and careful reasoning in university and professional subjects.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://mmmu-benchmark.github.io/)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 设计动机

Existing image-and-text evaluations lean toward everyday commonsense and struggle to cover heterogeneous professional images and university subject knowledge.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://mmmu-benchmark.github.io/)（访问 2026-11-08）

## Construction

### Question sources

Multidisciplinary undergraduates and coauthors manually collect questions from exams, quizzes, textbooks, lecture notes, and online resources; when necessary they write questions from professional knowledge.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://mmmu-benchmark.github.io/)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 出题/构建者背景

The collection stage of the paper involved 50 university students from different disciplines (including co-authors); the human-performance evaluation separately used 90 student experts in the corresponding disciplines, 3 per discipline. The two headcounts mean different things.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）

### Review process

Image hashing and text similarity are used to screen suspected duplicates, which the authors recheck; format, spelling, and answer quality are then cross-checked.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MMMU-Benchmark/MMMU/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Quality control

Image hashing and text similarity are used to screen suspected duplicates, which the authors recheck; format, spelling, and answer quality are then cross-checked.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MMMU-Benchmark/MMMU/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 去污染/泄漏控制

Questions were selected so that the answer is not easily obtained directly beside the source of the question; deduplication and hidden answers (the historical scheme) cannot guarantee that model training is free of contamination. The test answers were made public on 2026-02-12.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://mmmu-benchmark.github.io/)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 污染与已知争议

dev may be used for few-shot, validation is used to tune the prompt and the hyperparameters, and test now has public answers; it can no longer be labeled a hidden test. The number of image types has a documentation conflict, 30 in the paper and 32 in the README. The default EvalScope validation of 900 questions and the official test of 10500 questions must be kept on separate tracks.

[来源1](https://github.com/MMMU-Benchmark/MMMU/blob/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.16502)（访问 2026-11-08）

### 衍生与条目关系

MMMU-Pro is an independent enhanced version that filters out text shortcuts, expands the options, and adds a Vision mode; the official site also links Video-MMMU. The derivative is not treated as a result on the same denominator as the original MMMU.

[来源1](https://mmmu-benchmark.github.io/)（访问 2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://github.com/MMMU-Benchmark/MMMU/blob/main/README.md)（访问 2026-11-08）
