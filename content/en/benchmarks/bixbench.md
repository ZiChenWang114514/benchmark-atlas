# BixBench

> Benchmark Constitution, in-depth review: 2026-11-08. Supplemented from evidence; items that are not confirmed remain Unverified, and historical scores from papers are recorded separately from the live leaderboard.

BixBench requires an agent to run Python, R, or Bash inside a data capsule, complete a real computational-biology analysis, and answer a decidable question. Compared with question banks that test only a single biological function, it focuses on long-chain exploration and interpretation of results, but the reference answer still inherits the analytical choices of the original study. The most important version boundary comes from the official HF card: v1.5 is a revised dataset, with a large-scale re-review of questions whose original stems were insufficient. It cannot be treated merely as the name of a run configuration.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00096v3) (accessed 2026-11-07)

## Construction

### 题源

Experts write research capsules that include data and Jupyter analysis traces; after the model proposes candidate questions, they pass expert review and repeated filtering.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00096v3) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 构建人员

The hired analysts are all holders of a doctorate, or doctoral students, in bioinformatics and related fields, and biological data analysis is their primary research work.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096)（访问2026-11-08）

### 审核

Experts write research capsules that include data and Jupyter analysis traces; after the model proposes candidate questions, they pass expert review and repeated filtering.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00096v3) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 质控

People from the domain review a capsule and the candidate questions. Claude 3.5 Sonnet is run 3 times to screen for duplicates, the results are checked by hand, duplicates are deleted, and the process is then repeated. On 2025-09-23 HF acknowledged that many questions in the original edition did not give enough detail. v1.5 has been reviewed again at length and rewritten, and scores are expected to change.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futurehouse/BixBench/blob/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-08）

### 去污染及边界

The public HF record contains a per-question canary that forbids training; the paper includes deduplication of the questions. The canary depends on the party doing the training to filter actively, and it neither proves that upstream data already published never entered training nor removes the difference between v1.0 and v1.5.

[来源1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futurehouse/BixBench)（访问2026-11-08）；[来源2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096)（访问2026-11-08）

## Data format

### 规模与计数口径

The current README: 205 questions, drawn from 60 Jupyter notebooks that were actually published and from the associated data capsules; the counts should not be pooled with the later BixBench3.

[来源1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Future-House/BixBench/main/README.md)（访问2026-11-07）

### Difficulty

Open analysis tasks in computational biology; the expert trajectories listed in Table 1 of the paper average 4.2 hours and 106 lines of code, which only describes the sample, and is not an easy, medium, or hard label or a fixed budget for every model.

[来源1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096)（访问2026-11-08）

### 划分与公开范围

HF publishes the 205 questions as a single split named train. That name is the name of the release container. It does not mean that the paper trained on these questions. The questions, the reference answers, and the data capsules can be obtained. The README states that HF authentication is required at the time of access. This is not a contest with a hidden test.

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Open answers are scored by a model comparing them with the standard answer; a multiple-choice form is also provided; the number of runs and the agent environment must be retained.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00096v3) (accessed 2026-11-07)

Open answers are scored by a model comparing them with the standard answer; a multiple-choice form is also provided; the number of runs and the agent environment must be retained.

[Source 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00096) (accessed 2026-11-07); [Source 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00096v3) (accessed 2026-11-07)

### 官方评测入口

### 可比性边界
