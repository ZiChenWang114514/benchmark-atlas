# LabRobotBench

Evaluates whether agents can diagnose faults in a robotic wet lab, recover interrupted experiments, and schedule competing biological deadlines.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether frontier models can reason about physical space and complex biological workflows in a robotic wet lab, diagnose faults, recover interrupted experiments, and schedule competing deadlines.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The page notes that when a robot hits an error, experiments often stall until a person intervenes; agents that can diagnose problems and safely resume work could raise experimental throughput.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：V0.1 has 3 tasks; the benchmarks page lists 3 complete configurations.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Agents operate lab equipment through a simulated robot interface, finish interrupted workflows, and satisfy timing and sample-handling constraints.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：Simulated observations of robot state, tray and sample positions, chemical treatment steps, measuring instruments, and cold-storage limits.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：The pages are in English.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：The best mean score on the held-out subset is about 46.7%, showing a clear gap in recovery and deadline management.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：The page reports held-out subset results; some tasks are linked from gamowlabs.com/tasks, and fuller data require licensing.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/lab-robot-bench-v0-1) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/benchmarks) (accessed 2026-11-10)
