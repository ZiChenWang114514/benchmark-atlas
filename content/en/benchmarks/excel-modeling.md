# Excel Modeling Benchmark v1.1

> Benchmark Constitution, research snapshot 2026-11-07. The collection time is not the time a score was actually run, or the leaderboard update time.

What EMB evaluates is a financial workbook that can be recomputed in real Excel, with links among cells and with a change of scenario, rather than a standalone answer to one financial number. Template and Scratch put different requirements of delivery on the same task for an expert. Partial credit on the leaderboard reflects how far the work got, and it is not a rate of work that is ready to deliver.

## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Build investment-banking/private-equity financial models that can be opened and recalculated in real Excel, testing values, formulas, and usability.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Design background

Simple question answering or spreadsheet-cell operations cannot cover consistency across multiple tabs, switching among scenarios, and long chains of financial calculation.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Construction

### Question sources

Finance experts first build a Gold model, then strip the values and formulas to form a Template; every task, reference model, and rubric is created by experts and reviewed by peers.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 出题者背景

Finance-domain experts; acknowledgment of Andrew Schettino and the task authors and peer reviewers.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Review process

Experts created and peer-reviewed the business tasks, the Gold model, and the rubric.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Quality control

For each question, a financial expert first builds a Gold reference model, then removes the numbers and the formulas to obtain a Template. The question, the Gold, and the rubric are reviewed together by peers. After submission, a real Excel engine recomputes the workbook, and some tasks are checked by switching across scenarios such as Base, Bull, and Bear.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 污染控制

51 private held-out Test questions are reserved for the leaderboard on this page, in order to reduce overfitting; there is also 1 public question and 51 Validation questions that can be licensed. Private is not the same as having proved that the training set is completely free of contamination.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Equal-weight accuracy over seven task categories, including Template and Scratch scoring.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 评分

Recalculation is done first by the real Microsoft Excel engine; Template is matched on the values of the scoring units, and Scratch awards partial credit by a rubric over numbers, formulas, and presentation; the mean is taken inside each category first, and the seven categories have equal weight.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 提示与运行协议

A shared harness, with bash for reading and writing, execute checked by Excel recalculation, and submit for delivery; at most 3.5 hours each time.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### Saturation

The current leading score of 76.73% is an equal-weight partial score across seven categories and is not a perfect score; it is officially stated that a high score may still be unfinished work, and it cannot be treated as a rate of readiness for customers.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 污染与局限

The official source keeps Test private in order to reduce overfitting; v1.1 replaces the Scratch judge and repairs matching of sheet names, and results cannot be compared directly across versions. A high partial score does not represent a fully correct workbook that could be delivered to a client.

[一手资料1](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1)（访问 2026-11-07）

### 版本/衍生关系

EMB v1.0 is an archived older version; v1.1 adjusts the judge and the matching. Template and Scratch are two modes of the same questions, and the seven task families are not seven independent benchmarks.
