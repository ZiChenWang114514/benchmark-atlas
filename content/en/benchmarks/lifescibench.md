# LifeSciBench
> Verification: 2026-11-08. Existence verified; research snapshot;

An independent methods description and the complete preprint were found, supplying evidence where previously there was only a product promotional page. The LifeSciBench methods text for the 750 tasks covers seven categories of research workflow and uses fine-grained rubric scoring. The six workflows in the early product text and the later Gold v1 cannot, without explanation, be treated as exactly the same data snapshot. It evaluates single-turn research-task ability and does not directly prove a real acceleration of research and development.

## Design goals

**衡量能力**：Professional judgment under seven workflows: handling of scientific evidence, analysis, design optimization, scientific reasoning, verification operations, translation, and scientific communication; the benchmark examines incomplete evidence, scientific constraints, and output that can be acted on, and it is not the same as productivity in a real laboratory. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

**设计动机**：A single biological domain or a single sub-capability is not enough to represent the overall value of real research work. [S6](https://openai.com/index/introducing-new-capabilities-to-gpt-rosalind/)

## Construction

**题目/样本来源**：Workflows of working scientists were surveyed first, forming a taxonomy of seven classes, and 173 experts then wrote the questions, the attachments, and a rubric for each question; 750 tasks cover seven fields of biology. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

**构建者背景**：173 question writers, each required to hold a PhD in a relevant discipline and at least two years of in-service experience in biotechnology or the pharmaceutical industry; the fields include biochemistry, molecular biology, neuroscience, immunology, pharmacology, medicinal chemistry, and computational biology. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

**审查流程**：A task may be revised for any number of rounds; an accepted task receives on average six rounds of autonomous automatic review and at least two rounds of expert review; the checks cover agreement of the question with the rubric, the scientific challenge, the factual basis, and language and format, and they require agreement from at least 90% of domain experts. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

**质量控制**：A further 453 experts who did not take part in writing the questions verified them independently. Of them, 97% hold a doctorate or an equivalent degree, with an average of 12 years of experience. Overall endorsement is 98.3% for relevance to real practice, 98.1% for reasoning skill, 96.5% for a scientific basis, and 96.6% for overall usefulness. An independent expert is not the same as an independent institution retesting the models. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

## Evaluation

**指标**：The mean normalized rubric score gives every task equal weight. A task counts as passed when its rubric score is at least 70%. The bioRxiv abstract states that responses are averaged inside a task first and that the tasks are then averaged with equal weight. A score of 57.6% cannot be treated as a pass rate of 36.1%. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

**评分**：Points are added or subtracted for each rubric item and then divided by the total for the task, which yields a normalized score; automatic scoring and model-assisted scoring refer to the expert rubric, and a comparison of expert scores and automatic scores on a stratified sample is described. The specific grader model and the validation coefficient are Unverified. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/)

**提示/训练/运行设置**：Preprint §5 is a single turn that inputs the complete question and the attachments and allows unrestricted browsing on the network; follow-up questions, corrections, and iterative feedback are not allowed. The Astra release table separately takes the maximum over any effort, and it cannot automatically be treated as the same protocol. [S1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/b4299379-0a97-4ffa-8b9b-c3fbb299caa9/lifescibench_preprint.pdf) [S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744657v1) [S3](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/) [S4](https://openai.com/index/introducing-new-capabilities-to-gpt-rosalind/) [S5](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)
