## Design goals

### Abilities measured

Analysis, formatted presentation, and usable deliverables for professional knowledge work such as data science, product management, and corporate strategy.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

### Design background

Evaluating only short answers leaves out document analysis, integration across materials, and the quality of spreadsheets, presentations, and memos in real professional workflows.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

## Construction

### Question sources

Scenario source files mix real, augmented, and synthetic material, including Slack exports, email, tables, interviews, and market research; later weekly tasks may provide the same standard reference artifacts to every model.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking)（访问2026-11-07）

### 出题者背景

Built over several months by experts working in industry, with backgrounds that cover data science, product management, and corporate strategy. Official examples include Google, McKinsey & Company, and Boston Consulting Group. Collaboration by those institutions is not inferred from a background of employment there.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

### Review process

An explicit rubric is designed for the deliverables, and then combined with pairwise comparison of analysis quality and presentation quality.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

### Quality control

Official tasks are run independently and do not inherit a model's earlier submissions; later weeks may provide the same base-case file for all models. Objective rubric conditions and the paired quality of analysis and presentation are scored separately; the public Lite release provides the source_graph and traceability evidence chain.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）；[来源2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ArtificialAnalysis/AA-Briefcase-Lite)（访问2026-11-07）

### Decontamination

Scoring materials for the four formal scenarios remain private; the public fifth Lite example is only for trial use and is not included on the formal leaderboard.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

## Evaluation

### Metrics

Composite Elo; the binary-rubric pass rate, and the pairwise comparison of Analytical Quality and Presentation, are listed separately.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

### 评分

Binary rubric pass rates are converted into synthetic matches and combined with matches on analysis quality and presentation quality into the headline Elo; v1.1 fits reviewer quality with Crowd-BT in each scope. The rubric and the pairwise comparison each use a panel of three models, and each item is judged by one judge drawn by balanced sampling, rather than by a majority of three votes.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

### 运行设置

Each task runs independently, with no inheritance of self-generated artifacts from earlier tasks. The setting is a Stirrup/E2B offline sandbox, at most 500 turns, with code execution, a finish tool, and a give-up tool. Models that support vision can view images. Official tasks are run 1 time.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking)（访问2026-11-07）

### 官方脚本

https://github.com/ArtificialAnalysis/Stirrup

> Lite publishes the prompts, the grader/rubric, the source files, and example submissions; this does not support inferring that the formal private full implementation is open.

[来源1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ArtificialAnalysis/AA-Briefcase-Lite)（访问2026-11-07）；[来源2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）

### 可比性警示

v1.1 changes only the Elo fit and does not change the questions; Elo depends on the pool of opponents, the judge, and the fit, it cannot be interpreted as a percent-correct rate, and it cannot be added to or subtracted from the v1 Elo directly. Each task is run independently, and this is not a model working continuously for weeks.

[来源1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking#aa-briefcase)（访问2026-11-07）；[来源2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ArtificialAnalysis/AA-Briefcase-Lite)（访问2026-11-07）
