## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Long-horizon decision-making in real drug R&D scenarios, covering project decisions and scientific rigor, ADMET analysis, SAR and data interpretation, molecular design and synthesis, chemical reasoning, structure-based drug design and structural analysis, and biology and assay validity

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：Existing tools mostly cover structure prediction, molecule generation, or single ADMET endpoints, and lack long-horizon evaluation that places fragmented experimental evidence back into R&D context and decision trade-offs

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：In-house pharmaceutical R&D experts integrate assays, experimental results, and supported conclusions, then write scenario questions around project progression and key decision points

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Pharmaceutical R&D experts (described as in-house experts)

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Rubrics are based only on experiments that were conducted and their observed outcomes; the authors note that some questions may admit other reasonable answers while keeping the scoring basis traceable

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：Capability labels may overlap, with 200 subquestions mapped to 287 capability assignments; progressive disclosure prevents later-question leakage into earlier ones

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：Described as an internal benchmark; question text, splits, and decontamination protocol details are not disclosed

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：50 R&D scenarios with 200 questions; among seven capability dimensions, project decision-making and scientific rigor appear in 29.0%, ADMET analysis in 26.0%, SAR and data interpretation in 25.5%, molecular design and synthesis in 23.0%, chemical reasoning in 18.0%, structure-based drug design and structural analysis in 16.5%, and biology and assay validity in 5.5% (overlapping, so percentages do not sum to 100%)

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Open-ended decision and reasoning questions grounded in R&D scenarios, with multiple questions revealed in order within a scenario

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：Text; small-molecule cases may include SMILES and descriptions of crystal-structure interactions

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：English (paper and report text)

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：Long-horizon trade-offs along real drug pipelines; cases are shown ordered from hardest to easiest by mean model score

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：Internal benchmark; questions and splits are not public

Sources: [1](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1) [2](https://anewbt.com/) [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.20.752967v1.full.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)
