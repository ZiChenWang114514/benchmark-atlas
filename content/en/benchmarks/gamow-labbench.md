# Gamow LabBench

Uses 20 held-out tasks from real wet-lab archives to test whether agents can decide the next experiment, graded against binary criteria tied to the labs’ own decisions.

## Design goals

**Capabilities tested**：Tests whether agents can, from real wet-lab records at a decision point, commit to the next experiment or an evidence-supported conclusion, graded against binary criteria tied to the lab’s actual decision.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Gap in prior benchmarks**：The post and preprint emphasize that agents often interpret prior experiments well but struggle to choose, commit to, or rank the next experiment independently.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Construction

**Question sources**：Built from archives of a real drug-discovery program and an academic genomics lab; records available at the decision point are shown, while the lab’s interpretation and decision are withheld.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Author background**：Released by Gamow Labs.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Review process**：Biologists verify every task; tasks are hardened so solvers cannot pass them trivially.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Quality control**：Each task has about 20–22 binary criteria tied to visible evidence and the hidden record.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Decontamination**：Tasks come from internal lab records that the authors consider unlikely to be recalled from the open internet.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

## Data format

**Size**：20 held-out tasks; the benchmarks page also lists complete 20-task configurations.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Question types**：Free-form reports that commit to a decision: some tasks ask for ranked next experiments; others ask which condition, model, comparison, or claim the evidence supports.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Modalities**：Slide exports, figures, tables, scripts, and sequencing outputs among other lab records.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Languages**：English.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Difficulty**：The best agents pass about two in five criteria; criteria that require choosing, committing, or ranking pass about 21% of the time.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)

**Public and hidden split**：Held-out tasks; fuller data access is described via the company contact path.

Sources: [1](https://gamowlabs.com/labbench-benchmarking-ai-wet-lab-decisions) [2](https://gamowlabs.com/assets/labbench-preprint.pdf) (accessed 2026-11-10)
