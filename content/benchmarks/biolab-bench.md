## 设计目标

**要测的能力**：从用户意图到协议设计、设备无关 SOP、设备相关 SOP、机器代码与执行门控，评测智能体能否在真实自动化设备上走完湿实验链路。

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)

**现有基准的缺口**：仅看协议文本是否像真不够；需要检验生物学意图在可执行、可测试、可据反馈修订的工作流中是否保持一致。

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)

## 数据形式

**题量**：294道任务，源自98份金标准协议；难度分层 L1–L3（新闻稿）。

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)

**题型**：全链路评测：意图理解、协议设计、SOP 生成与翻译、机器代码、执行门控与湿实验反馈修订。

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)

**模态**：自然语言意图、协议文本、SOP、设备 SDK 代码与实验读出。

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)

**语言**：论文与新闻稿以英文为主。

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)

来源: [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31763) [2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgi-tech-and-shanghai-ai-laboratory-unveil-protopilot-and-biolab-bench-pioneering-physical-ai-for-life-sciences-302818081.html) [3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31763) (访问 2026-11-10)
